Preface

This little book is dedicated to the many precious souls who with the author were privileged in see and experience the early days of blessing of the "Latter Rain" outpouring at the old "power house," Azusa Street Mission. It is written with the hope and prayer that, where lost, the vision may be renewed to those who once shared with us in the glory of this "former house" of blessing; and also that it may, through this humble little medium, be told "to the generation following."

FRANK BARTLEMAN.

Los Angeles, Calif.

April. 1925
