# 第三十章　テキサス州ダラスでのしるしと不思議（その一）
CHAPTER XXX
SIGNS AND WONDERS AT DALLAS, TEXAS

（ここには、ダラスのF・F・ボズワース長老の報告が『ワード・アンド・ウィットネス』誌から転載されているが、著作権が残っているため訳していない）
// ここから「THE ACTS OF THE HOLY GHOST IN DALLAS」の前までは F. F. Bosworth（1958 年没）の報告。日本では著作権が残っている（2039 年ごろまで）ので、扱いが決まるまで訳さない。

(Reprinted from "Word and Witness,' from report of Elder F. F. Bosworth.)

Dallas, Tex., Aug. 11, 1912.

It has been about two weeks since our dear Sister Etter joined us in the meeting, and we give glory to God for the way he has used her every night in preaching and demonstrating the wonderful gospel of the Son of God.

The interest and power increases every night. Last night there were thousands of people in and around the tent. Sinners look on and weep, as they see the sick and afflicted healed by the power of God every night. Among those healed last night, before the great audience, was a mute, 36 years old, born deaf and dumb. He came from Oklahoma, and was instantly healed in the first meeting he attended last night. Sinners wept when they saw he could hear and shout praises to God.

Yesterday morning a wonderful miracle was wrought on a fifteen-year-old orphan boy--Emmett Martin. His right arm was paralyzed when he was one year old so that he has never been able to raise that arm above his head or to open and shut his hand. His other arm was in splints and in a sling, badly injured by falling off the street cara week ago. The bandages and splints were removed and the arm instantly and perfectly healed; and then when Sister Etter in the name of Jesus commanded him to stretch forth the paralyzed arm it was also instantly healed! He raised both hands above his head and clapped them together and shouted praises to God. He was gloriously saved at the same time, and was in the meeting again last night and testified before the great audience.

An invalid lady from Mesquite, Texas, was carried into the tent the other night. Had been an invalid four years with rheumatism all over her body; could not comb her own hair or raise her arms.

The lame, the blind, the deaf and dumb, the palsied, the paralytic, cancers, those suffering from operations, and others dying with incurable diseases have been wonderfully converted and healed by the power of God. Sinners are converted and flock to Jesus for salvation; and Christians are baptized with the Holy Ghost.

I wish all the saints in the pentecostal movement had a copy of Sister Etter's book. It is such a help to faith! There has been no such record written since the "Acts of the Apostles" recording such continuous victories by the Lord in our day over sin and sickness, as this book.

## ダラスにおける聖霊の行伝
THE ACTS OF THE HOLY GHOST IN DALLAS

(Reprinted from the Daily Times Herald.)
（『デイリー・タイムズ・ヘラルド』紙から転載）

At the apostolic meeting, hundreds of people are being healed --acres of people are at the tent every night.
使徒的な集会で、何百人もの人々がいやされている。天幕には毎晩、広い土地を埋めつくすほどの人々が来ている。
We don't have to believe what the papers have said about Mrs. Etter in California, Iowa, or Indiana; but go to the tent, see with your own eyes, and hear with your own ears the mighty works of God.
カリフォルニアやアイオワやインディアナで新聞がエター夫人について書いたことを、うのみにする必要はない。天幕へ行き、神の力あるわざを自分の目で見、自分の耳で聞けばよい。
People are coming from Oklahoma, Arkansas and Georgia to be healed.
オクラホマやアーカンソーやジョージアから、人々がいやされに来ている。

Elmer Hooper, 36 years old, from McAlester, Okla., born deaf and dumb, healed instantly.
オクラホマ州マカレスターのエルマー・フーパー、三十六歳。生まれつき耳が聞こえず口がきけなかったが、たちまちいやされた。

Mrs. Chelve Mallock, 3112 Boulevard St., Dallas, Texas, last stage of consumption, perfectly healed.
テキサス州ダラス、ブールバード通り三一一二番地のチェルヴ・マロック夫人。肺病の末期だったが、完全にいやされた。

Come on, "Bud," don't stand back on your old graveyard "theology" and miss the blessing.
さあ「相棒」、墓場のような古い「神学」の陰に引っ込んで、祝福を逃してはいけない。
God is in Dallas working miracles.
神はダラスにおられ、奇跡を行っておられる。
Everybody is coming, everybody praising God.
だれもが来て、だれもが神をほめたたえている。
Methodist, Baptist, Presbyterian, Campbellites and Catholics.
メソジストも、バプテストも、長老派も、キャンベル派も、カトリックもである。
If you stand off one hundred yards in the edge of the crowd you might suppose that John the Baptist had been resurrected, or John Bunyan, or John Knox, or John Calvin, or John Wesley, and the crowd had old time religion.
群衆の端の、九十メートルほど離れた所に立っていても、バプテスマのヨハネがよみがえったのかと思うかもしれない。あるいはジョン・バニヤンか、ジョン・ノックスか、ジャン・カルヴァンか、ジョン・ウェスレーがよみがえったのかと思うかもしれない。そして、群衆が昔ながらの信仰を取り戻したのかと思うだろう。

## テキサス州ダラスのリバイバルの続報
MORE ABOUT THE REVIVAL IN DALLAS, TEXAS

(Extracts reprinted from The Bridegroom's Messenger, Vol 5, No. 120.)
（『ブライドグルームズ・メッセンジャー』誌第五巻第百二十号から抜粋して転載）

God is still wonderfully displaying his power in Dallas, Texas, bearing witness to the preaching of the gospel "both with signs and wonders, and divers miracles and gifts in the Holy Ghost."
神は今もテキサス州ダラスで、驚くべきしかたで力を現しておられる。そして福音の宣教に、『しるしと不思議とさまざまな力あるわざとにより、また聖霊の賜物によって』あかしをしておられる（ヘブル二・四）。
// 後半は口語訳では「御旨に従い聖霊を各自に賜うことによって」。英文の gifts in the Holy Ghost に合わせて言い換えた。
Throngs pack the large tent at every service, many of them coming from other states.
集会のたびに、大天幕は群衆でぎっしりになる。その多くはほかの州から来ている。
Already great numbers of people have come from 100 to 2,000 miles, bringing their sick and afflicted.
すでにたいへん多くの人々が、病人や苦しむ人を連れて、百マイルから二千マイルの道のりをやって来た。
Letters are pouring in from all parts of the United States and Canada, as many as forty in a single day, inquiring about the meeting, and requesting prayer for healing.
合衆国とカナダの至る所から、一日に四十通もの手紙が殺到している。集会について問い合わせ、いやしのための祈りを求める手紙である。

A man came several hundred miles, suffering with three broken ribs, caused by a fall.
ある男が、数百マイルの道のりをやって来た。転んで肋骨を三本折り、苦しんでいた。
As impossible as it may seem, when hands were laid upon him and the prayer of faith was offered, immediately the soreness left, and the broken ribs, the ends of which had turned inward, came into place and knitted together spontaneously, and although a few minutes before he flinched from pain when Sister Etter laid her hands upon his side, after healing he could pound upon these ribs with his hands--the healing was perfect.
ありえないことのように思えるかもしれない。しかし、彼に手が置かれ、信仰による祈りがささげられると、たちまち痛みが去った。折れた肋骨は端が内側に曲がっていたが、ひとりでに元の位置に戻ってつながった。その数分前には、エター姉妹がわき腹に手を置くと、彼は痛みに身をすくめていた。しかしいやされた後には、その肋骨を自分の手でたたくことができた。いやしは完全だった。
He was also a backslider and was wonderfully reclaimed.
彼は信仰から離れた者でもあったが、驚くべきしかたで立ち返った。

A full-blooded Choctow Indian woman came from Oklahoma, a great sufferer for thirty-eight years with a running sore on her foot, caused by a cow stepping on it when she was sixteen years of age.
純血のチョクトー族のインディアンの女が、オクラホマからやって来た。十六歳のときに牛に足を踏まれ、それから三十八年のあいだ、その足の膿の出る傷にひどく苦しんでいた。
// 英文の Choctow は原本のまま（ページ画像で確かめた）。Choctaw（チョクトー族）と読んだ。
It was a mass of proud flesh, and the odor was almost unbearable.
傷は盛り上がった肉の塊で、においはほとんど耐えられないほどだった。
Was kept awake at nights and could not bear the weight of the covers on this foot.
夜も眠れず、その足に掛かる布団の重みにも耐えられなかった。
By the laying on of hands, God instantly took away all pain, and the foot in now healing up.
手を置くことによって、神はたちまち痛みをすべて取り去ってくださった。足は今、治りつつある。
// 英文の in now は原本の誤植（ページ画像で確かめた）。is now と読んだ。

Last Sunday, Brother Bosworth baptized thirty-nine more in water and the power of God was present in a mighty way.
この前の日曜日、ボズワース兄弟がさらに三十九人に水のバプテスマを授けた。神の力が大いに臨んでいた。
A great crowd witnessed the scene.
大群衆がその光景を見守った。

More than one hundred and twenty-five have received the baptism in the Holy Spirit during the past two months, eight received the baptism on last Saturday night, twelve one Sunday.
この二か月のあいだに、百二十五人以上が聖霊のバプテスマを受けた。この前の土曜日の夜には八人が、ある日曜日には十二人がバプテスマを受けた。
There have been as many as fifty baptised in the Holy Spirit in one week, many receiving it in their homes.
一週間に五十人もが聖霊のバプテスマを受けたこともあり、多くの人は自分の家で受けた。
For two months the mighty power and presence of God has been present in these services in a marvellous way.
この二か月、これらの集会には、神の大いなる力と臨在が驚くべきしかたで現れている。

## テキサス州ダラスで増し加わる神の不思議
INCREASING WONDERS OF GOD IN DALLAS, TEXAS

The meetings are increasing in interest and power, and the people are coming from many states.
集会は関心も力も増し加わっており、人々は多くの州からやって来る。
A man got here yesterday morning, coming from Indianapolis, Ind., on purpose to attend this meeting.
きのうの朝には、ひとりの男が、この集会に出るためにわざわざインディアナ州インディアナポリスから着いた。
Another from the Pacific coast, some from Illinois, Michigan, and other states.
太平洋岸から来た人もおり、イリノイやミシガンやほかの州から来た人々もいる。
Many drive forty and fifty miles: and they were here yesterday from Galveston, Houston, and many other towns in Texas.
四十マイル、五十マイルの道のりを自分で駆って来る人も多い。きのうは、ガルベストンやヒューストンや、テキサスのほかの多くの町から人々が来ていた。

On August 12th three men brought a man dying with consumption and fistula, two hundred miles in a baggage car on a cot.
八月十二日、三人の男が、肺病と瘻孔で死にかけているひとりの男を連れて来た。男を簡易寝台に乗せ、荷物車で二百マイルの道のりを運んで来たのである。
He came from Mercury, Texas, and looked like a dead man when they carried him into the tent on the cot.
彼はテキサス州マーキュリーから来た。簡易寝台に乗せられて天幕に運び込まれたときは、死人のように見えた。
He was in the very last stages of tuberculosis, and nothing much but bones.
結核のいちばん末期で、ほとんど骨ばかりになっていた。
When prayed for, the power came and he jumped from the cot and ran up and down before the people, praising God.
祈ってもらうと力が臨み、彼は簡易寝台から跳び起きた。そして神をほめたたえながら、人々の前を走り回った。
He returned home sitting up like other passengers, and is gaining four pounds a week, and the fistula was healed over the next morning, and never had to be dressed again.
彼はほかの乗客と同じように座って家に帰り、今では一週間に二キロ近く体重が増えている。瘻孔は翌朝にはふさがり、それからは一度も手当てをする必要がなかった。
The country was stirred, and about twenty-five more have come from that vicinity.
その地方一帯が揺り動かされ、その近くからさらに二十五人ほどが来た。
Night before last a delegation from that county came with two deaf and dumb mutes.
おとといの夜には、その郡からの一団が、耳が聞こえず口のきけないふたりを連れて来た。
One was thirty-four years old and the other a beautiful looking young lady of seventeen years.
ひとりは三十四歳、もうひとりは十七歳の美しい若い婦人だった。
God opened the ears of both.
神はふたりの耳を開いてくださった。
The young lady was saved and healed at the same time, and is very happy.
若い婦人は救われると同時にいやされ、たいへん喜んでいる。
They were both at our home this morning, and I played on the organ for them.
けさ、ふたりは私たちの家に来ていた。私はふたりのためにオルガンを弾いた。
Many others have been healed of deafness.
ほかにも多くの人が、耳が聞こえないのをいやされた。

Yesterday was the most wonderful day I ever saw in this work.
きのうは、私がこの働きで見たうちで最もすばらしい日だった。
God came with the melting and slaying power.
神は、心を溶かし、人を打ち倒す力をもって来られた。
Twenty-one were struck down like Paul by the power of God, and lay from one to ten hours.
二十一人が、パウロのように神の力によって打ち倒され、一時間から十時間のあいだ横たわっていた。
They had wonderful visions of heaven and of Jesus and all came out with shining faces and filled with the love of God.
その人々は天国とイエスのすばらしい幻を見た。そしてみな、顔を輝かせ、神の愛に満たされて我に返った。
Twelve of these received the Holy Spirit and spoke in tongues for the first time.
そのうちの十二人は聖霊を受け、初めて異言を語った。
Never saw such power displayed.
これほどの力の現れは、見たことがない。
It looked like a battlefield to see them fall and lie as dead God said, "The slain of the Lord shall be many."
人々が倒れて死人のように横たわるのを見ると、まるで戦場のようだった。神は『主に打ち倒される者は多い』と言われた（イザヤ六六・一六）。
// 英文の as dead と God のあいだに句点がないのは原本のまま（ページ画像で確かめた）。口語訳は「主に殺される者は多い」。この本の訳語 the slain of the Lord＝主に打ち倒される者に合わせた。
Sinners look on and weep as they see the wonderful works of God.
罪人たちは神のすばらしいみわざを見て、泣いている。

A woman seventy-five years old suffering with rheumatism twenty years, was brought two hundred miles, and was healed in the first service.
二十年のあいだリウマチに苦しんでいた七十五歳の女が、二百マイルの道のりを連れて来られ、最初の集会でいやされた。
She came through the healing of the consumptive above referred to.
彼女が来たのは、前に述べた肺病の男がいやされたからである。

A boy totally deaf from birth was brought by his father from Martinsburg, Texas.
生まれつき耳がまったく聞こえない少年が、父親に連れられてテキサス州マーティンズバーグから来た。
God healed him perfectly, and his parents have written back to us that his hearing is perfect, and he is learning to talk.
神は少年を完全にいやしてくださった。両親から手紙が来て、耳は完全に聞こえ、話すことを覚えているところだ、と書いてあった。

A woman from Palmer, Texas, was healed of pellegra and filled with the Spirit at the same time.
テキサス州パーマーから来た女が、ペラグラをいやされ、同時に御霊に満たされた。
// 英文の pellegra は原本のまま（ページ画像で確かめた）。pellagra（ペラグラ）と読んだ。
She was given up by the physicians.
医者たちに見放されていた女である。
She is still here in the meeting and is well.
彼女は今も集会に来ており、元気である。
A man from Blueridge, Texas, sixty miles from Dallas, had suffered ten years with cancer all over one side of his face and neck.
ダラスから六十マイル離れたテキサス州ブルーリッジの男は、顔と首の片側一面に広がった癌に、十年のあいだ苦しんでいた。
Suffered so he had to be taken from the meeting.
苦しみがひどく、集会から連れ出さなければならないほどだった。
Could not talk on account of the awful suffering from moving his jaw.
あごを動かすとひどく痛むので、話すこともできなかった。
When Sister Etter prayed for him the power came and he was healed.
エター姉妹が彼のために祈ると、力が臨み、彼はいやされた。
The pain and all the burning and stiffness left instantly, and he could turn his head in any direction without pain.
痛みも、焼けるような感じやこわばりもすべて、たちまち去った。頭をどの方向に向けても痛まなくなった。
He got up on the altar and preached to the people.
彼は祭壇の所に立って、人々に説教した。
His friends told me yesterday that the cancer was healing up and he is getting well.
きのう彼の友人たちが、癌は治りつつあり、彼は元気になってきている、と私に話してくれた。

The night before last a Catholic lady came into the meeting for the first time.
おとといの夜、カトリックの婦人が初めて集会に来た。
She was deaf in both ears and could not hear a sound as the choir sang.
彼女は両耳が聞こえず、聖歌隊が歌っても何の音も聞こえなかった。
She was instantly healed and could hear an ordinary conversation.
彼女はたちまちいやされ、ふつうの会話が聞こえるようになった。
Saturday night a lady was brought from Beaumont, Texas, dying with tumor.
土曜日の夜には、腫瘍で死にかけている婦人が、テキサス州ボーモントから連れて来られた。
The doctors said she would not live forty-eight hours.
医者たちは、四十八時間ももたないだろうと言っていた。
She was instantly saved, healed and baptized in the Spirit, and leaped and praised God before the people.
彼女はたちまち救われ、いやされ、御霊のバプテスマを受けた。そして人々の前で跳び上がり、神をほめたたえた。
Sinners look on and weep, and then make their way to the altar.
罪人たちはそれを見て泣き、それから祭壇の前へ進み出る。

I said that Sunday was the most wonderful day I had ever seen.
日曜日は私が見たうちで最もすばらしい日だった、と私は書いた。
Last night was still more wonderful.
ゆうべは、さらにすばらしかった。
No tongue or pen could describe that meeting.
あの集会は、口でも筆でも言い表せない。
Three deaf and dumb mutes, fifty-four, thirty-four, and seventeen years old, all strangers to each other, hugged, kissed, wept and shouted for about a half hour, because God had opened all their ears, gave them their voices, and saved them all.
耳が聞こえず口のきけない三人がいた。五十四歳と三十四歳と十七歳で、互いにまったく見知らぬ者どうしだった。神が三人の耳をみなお開きになり、声をお与えになり、三人とも救ってくださった。それで三人は三十分ほど抱き合い、口づけし、泣き、叫んだ。
The great audience looked on and went. and as many as could crowd into the vacant space at the front of the platform sought God for salvation, healing and the baptism.
大会衆はそれを見て泣いた。そして、壇の前の空いた所に詰めかけられるだけの人々が詰めかけ、救いと、いやしと、バプテスマとを神に求めた。

Many were struck down by the power of God and had wonderful visions of Jesus, and many received the baptism in the Holy Ghost as at Pentecost.
多くの人が神の力によって打ち倒され、イエスのすばらしい幻を見た。また多くの人が、ペンテコステの日のように聖霊のバプテスマを受けた。
Some are stricken down in their homes.
自分の家で打ち倒される人々もいる。
I wish vou could see how these deaf and dumb mutes looked at the choir, making signs to each other that they enjoyed the singing and the instruments.
耳が聞こえず口のきけなかった人々が聖歌隊を見つめ、歌と楽器を楽しんでいることを身ぶりで伝え合っていた。その様子を、読者にも見せたかった。

The delegation that came with the deaf and dumb mutes returned with them this morning, saying that they were going to bring another mute, and perhaps some other afflicted ones.
その人々と一緒に来た一団は、けさ彼らを連れて帰った。もうひとり口のきけない人を連れて来る、ほかにも苦しむ人々を連れて来るかもしれない、と言っていた。
A preacher last night that came with them got up and told how he had known the deaf and dumb young lady from birth and loved her as his own daughter.
ゆうべ、一団と一緒に来た説教者が立ち上がって話した。あの耳が聞こえず口のきけなかった若い婦人を生まれたときから知っていて、自分の娘のように愛してきた、と。

## ダラスでのいやしの奇跡
MIRACLES OF HEALING IN DALLAS

(Reprinted from The Latter Rain Evangel of Chicago, U. S. A., October, 1912.)
（シカゴの『ラター・レイン・エバンジェル』誌、一九一二年十月号から転載）

God is truly working in a wonderful way in Dallas, Texas, in the meetings conducted by Mrs. Woodworth-Etter and the pastor.
テキサス州ダラスで、ウッドワース・エター夫人と牧師が導いている集会において、神はまことに驚くべきしかたで働いておられる。
During the first week in September about fifty were baptized in the Holy Spirit, besides many bright conversions and miraculous healings.
九月の第一週には、五十人ほどが聖霊のバプテスマを受けた。そのほかに、輝かしい回心と奇跡的ないやしが数多くあった。

We quote the following from a private letter received from an eye-witness under date of August 22nd:
目撃者から受け取った八月二十二日付けの私信から、次の部分を引用する。

"There are about five thousand people in attendance every night and on Sunday nights many more.
「毎晩、五千人ほどが集まっています。日曜日の夜には、さらに多く集まります。
The sick and afflicted are coming from all parts of Texas, and some have come from Oklahoma, Alabama, Georgia and Louisiana.
病人や苦しむ人々が、テキサスの至る所から来ています。オクラホマ、アラバマ、ジョージア、ルイジアナから来た人もいます。
They are coming on trains, covered wagons, and every way they can.
列車で、幌馬車で、あらゆる手段を使ってやって来ます。
Oh, the pitiful sights! How they make my heart ache! Sometimes it is more than I can bear when I see as many as four or five in one service nearly eaten up with cancer or consumption and given up by all earthly aid, and as a last resort they come to the Lord.
ああ、なんと痛ましい光景でしょう！　どれほど私の心が痛むことでしょう！　ときには、耐えられないほどです。一つの集会に、癌や肺病にほとんど食い尽くされ、この世のあらゆる助けに見放された人が、四人も五人もいるのを見るときです。その人々は最後の頼みとして、主のもとに来るのです。

"The first night I was here, there were four brought in on cots in a dying condition, several in roller chairs and many afflicted who were able to walk.

LETTER FROM AN ENGLISH EDITOR

WONDERS OF GOD IN DALLAS, TEXAS

During this revival hundreds have been saved and healed. The blind have seen, the deaf have heard, the dumb have spoken; broken limbs have been restored, incurable diseases like cancer, consumption and pellagra have been healed. The word of God has gone forth in power, and the Lord has confirmed it with "signs following."

About twelve hundred have received the baptism of the Holy Ghost as on the day of Pentecost, each one speaking in other tongues as the Spirit gave them utterance. During our month's stay here we have seen the dear Lord mightily manifest His healing power at different times. A brother came down from Missouri, very far gone with consumption. He had taken to his bed because he was so weak, but the Lord told him to come to Dallas and he would heal him. As Sister Etter laid hands on him, rebuking the disease in the name of Jesus, it went at once and the brother was able to give an overflowing testimony the next day.

A sister came down from St. Paul who had been an invalid for two years, ever since the birth of her baby. She had all kinds of internal trouble, but the prayer of faith by Sister Etter put everything right, and the Lord raised her up from the couch on which she lay. She ran up and down the tabernacle praising the Lord. In her testimony she said that previously she had been so ill that it was only with difficulty she could move from her bed from a chair in her room. She stayed a little while longer in Dallas, during which she became strengthened in spirit, soul and body, and the Lord baptised her with the Holy Ghost. She writes from St. Paul telling how well she is.

Another sister came down with the worst case of goitre we have ever seen. She had been under many physicians and a specialist, and her case was pronounced incurable. When her mother decided to bring her to Dallas, the doctor said the train journey would probably kill her. On the first night she came Sister Etter called on the name of Jesus and the disease was cured. (We notice that in almost every case Sister Etter dealt with the disease as if she was dealing with the devil himself.) At once the sister was delivered, the pain left, and she was able to exercise her swollen neck. It seemed as though the swelling subsided considerably at once, although the swelling had not all gone before she left Dallas, she is able to give a very blessed testimony. For the first time in a long time she was able to sleep peacefully, she was able to walk 32 blocks to and from the meetings every day, whereas before she came she could not walk more than two blocks, and that with difficulty.

At another time a man who looked as though he was dying, suffering internally with appendicitis, came into the meeting, the Lord not only healed his body, but saved his soul. Salvation means the double cure, and really means WHOLENESS and you cannot limit it to the part of the being, as we have often done in past times.

Sister Etter has gone to San Antonio, starting meetings there the first of January. We hear the Lord is blessedly confirming His word with signs following. Her farewell service took place on the last Sunday of the old year, and was one of the most blessed services we have ever attended. Many times while giving her message, she was so overcome with emotion she had to stop. Especially pathetic was her farewell to Bros. Bosworth and Birdsall, "these two dear boys," as this mother in Israel called them. She appealed to all to stand by them. The power of God seemed especially on this dear sister that night, and as she laid hands on sick.ones and seekers, they one and all seemed to come mightly under the power of God, becoming prostrated before the Lord.

Yours in the boundless love of Jesus.

STANLEY H. FRODSHAM.
