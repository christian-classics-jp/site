# 第三十章　テキサス州ダラスでのしるしと不思議（その二）
CHAPTER XXX
SIGNS AND WONDERS AT DALLAS, TEXAS

## ダラスの集会のさらに詳しい報告
FURTHER DETAILS OF THE DALLAS MEETING

## テキサス州ダラスにおける神の大いなる力
THE MIGHTY POWER OF GOD AT DALLAS, TEXAS

(By Carrie Judd Montgomery, Editor Triumphs of Faith, Oakland, California.)
（カリフォルニア州オークランド、『トライアンフス・オブ・フェイス』誌編集者、キャリー・ジャッド・モンゴメリー）

My husband and myself are just leaving Dallas, Texas, after a most interesting time at the meetings which are being held here in this city at the large Tabernacle (recently built to take place of the tent).
夫と私は、テキサス州ダラスを発とうとしているところである。この町の大きな幕屋（天幕に代わって最近建てられた）で開かれている集会で、たいへん興味深い時を過ごした。
Mrs. Etter has been there five months holding meetings, and will remain until the first of the year, when she goes to San Antonio, Texas, for a series of meetings.
エター夫人はそこで五か月のあいだ集会を開いてきた。年の初めまでとどまり、それからテキサス州サンアントニオへ行って、一連の集会を開く。

Mrs. Etter is an old-time friend of ours.
エター夫人は、私たちの古くからの友人である。
When we first knew her she was in the midst of a very remarkable revival work on the Pacific coast, where many thousands were saved, and many were healed.
私たちが初めて夫人と知り合ったとき、夫人は太平洋岸で、たいへん目ざましいリバイバルの働きのさなかにあった。そこでは何千人もが救われ、多くの人がいやされた。
We were delighted to meet each other again, after the lapse of many years.
私たちと夫人は、長い年月を経て再会し、喜び合った。
She extended to us a most loving welcome, and gave us seats on the platform by her side.
夫人は私たちを心から温かく迎え、壇の上の自分の隣に席を用意してくれた。

We have already published reports of these Dallas meetings, which we knew were reliable, but it has been a joy to attend them for ourselves, and to witness the mighty power of God convicting and converting sinners, healing sick bodies, and baptising saints with the Holy Ghost.
私たちはこれまでにも、このダラスの集会の報告を誌上に載せてきた。それが信頼できる報告であることもわかっていた。しかし、自分たちで集会に出て、神の大いなる力を目の当たりにしたのは喜びだった。その力は、罪人に罪を示して回心させ、病んだ体をいやし、聖徒に聖霊のバプテスマを授けていた。
We could only make it possible to remain for a few days, but in that length of time we saw much that we shall never forget.
私たちは数日しかとどまれなかったが、そのあいだに、決して忘れられないものを数多く見た。
As we entered the Tabernacle on Saturday evening, we saw a crowd of rejoicing ones at the front, and we learned later that a deaf and dumb man, about sixty years of age, had just been saved and instantly healed by the power of God through Mrs. Etter's command of faith, as she bade the deaf and dumb demons to depart in Jesus' name.
土曜日の夕方に幕屋に入ると、前のほうに喜び合う人々の群れが見えた。あとで聞くと、耳が聞こえず口のきけない六十歳ほどの男が、ちょうど救われ、たちまちいやされたところだった。エター夫人が信仰によって命じ、耳が聞こえず口のきけない悪霊どもにイエスの名によって去れと言うと、神の力によってそうなったのである。
Another man who had been healed of deafness was pointed out to us, and as he was singing a hymn in unison with the others. with eyes closed, it was evident how well he could hear.
耳が聞こえないのをいやされた別の男を、人々が教えてくれた。彼は目を閉じたまま、ほかの人々と声を合わせて賛美歌を歌っていたので、どれほどよく聞こえているかは明らかだった。
// 英文の the others. with の句点は原本の誤植（ページ画像で確かめた）。読点と読んだ。
We saw one lady who had been stone deaf, but after prayer was offered, the ears which had seemed dead began to have life in them, and she could hear loud sounds near her.
まったく耳の聞こえなかったある婦人にも会った。祈りがささげられた後、死んだようだった耳に生気が戻りはじめ、近くの大きな音が聞こえるようになっていた。
Some people are healed at once, and others gradually, but Mrs. Etter feels that if they have been receptive as she has prayed "the prayer of faith" for them, that they have only to hold on in faith and continue to praise God on the authority of His Word, and the symptoms will surely pass away.
たちまちいやされる人もいれば、少しずつよくなる人もいる。しかしエター夫人はこう考えている。夫人がその人々のために『信仰による祈』（ヤコブ五・一五）をささげたとき、その人々が素直に受け入れていたとする。その場合、あとは信仰によって持ちこたえ、神のみことばを根拠に神をほめたたえ続けさえすればよい。そうすれば症状は必ず消えていく。
There really are so many healings day by day, that only the more remarkable ones attract attention.
実際、日々あまりに多くのいやしがあるので、とりわけ目ざましいものだけが人の目を引くのである。

Mrs. Etter's greatest concern is to have people "get right with God" in their souls, and then she tells them they will be healed.
エター夫人がいちばん心にかけているのは、人々が魂において「神との関係を正しくする」ことである。そのうえで夫人は、あなたがたはいやされる、と告げる。
When they come to her with more desire for physical healing than for the spiritual healing, she refuses to pray for the healing of their bodies until their souls come into right relations with God.
霊のいやしよりも体のいやしを求めて夫人のもとに来る人々には、魂が神と正しい関係に入るまで、体のいやしのために祈ることを断る。
This undoubtedly is one great reason of her success, and another reason is that she believes in working for the unifying of all the members of the body of Christ, and therefore she does not preach mere theories, but holds up a living Christ, receiving all who are honest in their hearts and purposes towards Him, even if they do not yet see the truth just as she teaches it.
これは疑いなく、夫人の働きが実を結んでいる大きな理由の一つである。もう一つの理由は、キリストのからだのすべての肢体を一つにするために働くべきだと、夫人が信じていることである。だから夫人はただの理論を説かず、生きておられるキリストを高く掲げる。そして、キリストに対して心と志の正直な人をみな受け入れる。たとえその人々が、夫人の教えるとおりにはまだ真理を見ていなくてもである。
She also avoids laying stress upon certain words or expressions, with which the enemy is trying to cause divisions in the body of Christ.
また夫人は、特定の言葉や言い回しを強調することを避けている。敵はそれを使って、キリストのからだに分裂を起こそうとしているからである。
Therefore there is no contention or strife in these meetings, but love and unity.
それでこの集会には、争いや不和がなく、愛と一致がある。

It is interesting to note that Mrs. Etter teaches healing in the Atonement (in the same way that we are taught by the Holy Spirit Himself many years ago).
エター夫人が、あがないの中にいやしがあると教えていることは興味深い（何年も前に、私たちが聖霊ご自身から教えられたのと同じである）。
She also encourages all the saints to press on for more power from God to do the miraculous works which Jesus said believers should do in His name.
また夫人は、すべての聖徒に、神からさらに力をいただくよう求め進むことを勧めている。信じる者がイエスの名によって行う、とイエスが言われた奇跡のわざを、行うための力である。

Mrs. Etter is one that speaks with no uncertain sound, and we have never seen any one else rebuke disease and demons with such Heaven-sent authority, and power.
エター夫人は、はっきりした音で語る人である。病と悪霊を、これほど天から授かった権威と力とをもってしかりつける人を、私たちはほかに見たことがない。

It brought a new wave of spiritual joy to our heart to hear the way in which these "cruel demons" were ordered to depart.
この「残酷な悪霊ども」が去れと命じられるのを聞いて、私たちの心には霊の喜びが新たに押し寄せた。
Perhaps it is needless to say here that when people get their eyes on the instrument that God uses, instead of upon Himself, they do not receive healing.
ここで言うまでもないことかもしれないが、人々が神ご自身ではなく、神がお用いになる器に目を向けるなら、いやしは受けられない。
While on the other hand, those whose gaze of faith is upon the Saviour alone often receive healing, through the application in faith of the anointed handkerchiefs or tracts sent out from these meetings.
その一方で、信仰の目を救い主だけに注いでいる人々は、しばしばいやしを受けている。この集会から送り出される、油を注いだハンカチや小冊子を信仰によって当てることによってである。
Some periodicals have ridiculed this means of reaching the sick, but God is wonderfully using them just the same, and as He wrought special miracles by the hands of Paul (see Acts xix:11, 12) He is doing the same now for those who trust Him, for so many of God's children are sick everywhere that the need is most urgent and the unbelief is so great that often in isolated places those suffering saints cannot get one near at hand to offer for them "the prayer of faith."
この方法で病人に手を伸ばすことをあざ笑う雑誌もある。しかし神は、それでも驚くべきしかたでハンカチや小冊子をお用いになっている。神はパウロの手によって異常な力あるわざをなさったが（使徒一九・一一〜一二）、今も、神に信頼する人々のために同じことをしておられる。至る所で、神の子供たちのたいへん多くが病んでおり、必要はきわめて差し迫っているからである。不信仰もたいへん大きい。そのため人里離れた所では、苦しむ聖徒たちのために『信仰による祈』（ヤコブ五・一五）をささげてくれる人が、近くに見つからないことがよくある。

Mrs. Etter preaches the gospel in great simplicity and power, backing up all her remarks by quotations from the Word of God.
エター夫人は、たいへん素朴に、力強く福音を説く。語ることはすべて、神のみことばを引いて裏づける。
The altar services are very remarkable, as saints and sinners gather around in deep earnestness seeking God for their individual needs.
祭壇での集いは、たいへん目ざましい。聖徒も罪人も、深い真剣さをもって祭壇のまわりに集まり、それぞれの必要を神に求める。
The power of God falls upon them, and it is wonderful to note the changes that come over the faces of the seeking ones, as the light dawns upon their souls.
神の力がその人々の上に下る。求める人々の魂に光が差しはじめるとき、その顔に表れる変化を見るのはすばらしい。
The power of God often prostrates them, and even little children are seen "under the power;" apparently unconscious to all but God, and with their little faces shining like angels.
神の力は、しばしばその人々を打ち倒す。小さな子供たちさえ、「力に打たれて」いるのが見られる。神のほかには何も意識していないようで、その小さな顔は御使のように輝いている。

There were people present from many different states in the Union.
集会には、合衆国の多くの州から来た人々がいた。

The night we left there were three remarkable cases of healing; one of goitre, one of cancer, and one of deafness.
私たちが発った夜には、目ざましいいやしが三つあった。甲状腺腫と、癌と、耳の聞こえないのとである。
The lady with goitre was suffering very much with the choking or suffocating sensation occasioned by it.
甲状腺腫の婦人は、それによる、のどを締めつけられるような、息の詰まるような感じにひどく苦しんでいた。
Mrs. Etter commanded the enemy to depart, in Jesus' name, and soon we saw her turning her head freely from side to side, while the swelling seemed mostly, if not entirely gone.
エター夫人がイエスの名によって、敵に去れと命じた。するとまもなく、婦人が頭を左右に自由に動かすのが見えた。腫れはすっかりとは言わないまでも、ほとんど引いたようだった。
The pain and swelling of the man's cancer was also taken away in a few minutes.
癌の男の痛みと腫れも、数分のうちに取り去られた。

It is stated that about three hundred and fifty have received the Pentecostal baptism, with the sign of speaking with new tongues.
聞くところによると、三百五十人ほどが、新しい言葉を語るしるしを伴うペンテコステのバプテスマを受けたという。

There is usually a great solemnity in the meetings, and the faces of the people are very earnest.
集会にはたいてい深いおごそかさがあり、人々の顔はたいへん真剣である。
With the exception of the altar service, where many are often praising or praying at once, the meetings are conducted very quietly.
祭壇での集いでは、多くの人が一度に賛美したり祈ったりしていることがよくある。しかしそれを除けば、集会はたいへん静かに進められる。
All tendency to wildfire or fanaticism is entirely discountenanced.
燃え広がる野火のような熱狂や狂信に向かう傾きは、すべてきっぱりと退けられている。

Perhaps the explanation for the solemnity of these meetings is that there is continual teaching about the soon coming of the Lord for His Bride, and exhortations to get ready.
この集会がおごそかなのは、おそらく、主が花嫁を迎えに間もなく来られることがたえず教えられ、備えをするようにと勧められているからだろう。
One dear brother, Rev. If. C. Mears, who has preached the gospel for over forty years, came to fight the work, but became convinced that it was of God; he received the baptism of the Holy Spirit with the sign of tongues, and afterwards God gave him the most wonderful visions of Heaven, and of what the Lord is preparing for His people, and also visions of the coming of the Lord.

It is most inspiring to listen to the revelations of the coming of the Lord which have been vouchsafed to this dear brother.
この愛する兄弟に与えられた、主の来臨についての啓示を聞くと、大いに奮い立たされる。
His spirit is so loving and gentle, he reminds us of what the Lord Jesus said about Nathaniel, "Behold an Israelite indeed, in whom there is no guile."
彼の心はたいへん愛に満ち、柔和なので、主イエスがナタナエルについて言われた言葉を思い起こさせる。『見よ、あの人こそ、ほんとうのイスラエル人である。その心には偽りがない』（ヨハネ一・四七）。

## 約束を自分のものとする
CLAIMING THE PROMISE

"Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you." Luke 10.19.
『わたしはあなたがたに、へびやさそりを踏みつけ、敵のあらゆる力に打ち勝つ権威を授けた。だから、あなたがたに害をおよぼす者はまったく無いであろう』（ルカ一〇・一九）。

At a recent meeting at the time just after the call for all needing prayer to come to the altar had been made, Sister Etter had been praying for many round the altar and God wonderfully healed them all, and as she was praying for a lady there was a scorpion laying two inches away from her hand.
最近の集会でのことである。祈りの必要な人はみな祭壇の前に出るように、という招きがなされたすぐ後だった。エター姉妹は祭壇のまわりで多くの人のために祈っていた。神は驚くべきしかたで、その人々をみないやしてくださった。姉妹がひとりの婦人のために祈っていると、姉妹の手から五センチほどの所に、さそりが一匹いた。

She saw it and went to knock it off and it stung her.
姉妹はそれに気づき、払いのけようとして、刺された。
The pain seemed to run all through her and go to her heart.
痛みは全身を走り、心臓にまで届くように思われた。
The finger got red, and the Lord showed her it was a fatal and poisonous sting, and nothing but the Lord could help her.
指は赤くなった。主は姉妹に、これは命取りになる毒のある刺し傷で、主のほかには何も姉妹を助けられない、と示された。
A brother killed it, and several of the saints saw it, and went right to praying.
ひとりの兄弟がさそりを殺した。何人かの聖徒がそれを見て、すぐに祈りはじめた。
She claimed the promise that we should have power over serpents, and all fear left her.
姉妹は、私たちにへびに打ち勝つ権威が与えられるという約束を、自分のものとした。すると恐れはすべて去った。

The people were waiting for her to heal them.
人々は、姉妹がいやしてくれるのを待っていた。
She hung on to the Lord, and His promises, and the pain began to stop and she went right on praying for the sick, and nothing was left but the mark of the sting.
姉妹は主と主の約束にすがりついた。すると痛みが治まりはじめ、姉妹はそのまま病人のために祈り続けた。刺された跡のほかには、何も残らなかった。
The hurt was perfectly healed.
傷は完全にいやされた。

A brother came to these meetings who had been a holiness preacher for eight years past; he had been fighting this work for about three years; finally the Lord showed him he had to make a final decision, and he decided to walk in the Light.
この八年ホーリネスの説教者をしてきた兄弟が、この集会に来た。彼は三年ほど、この働きと戦ってきた。ついに主は、最終的な決断をしなければならないことを彼に示された。彼は光の中を歩むことに決めた。
He, his wife, and four sons, and daughter, and her husband, all received the baptism of the Holy Ghost, since they attended these meetings.
この集会に来てから、彼と妻と四人の息子と、娘とその夫とが、みな聖霊のバプテスマを受けた。
The power of God often comes on two of the sons, and while prostrated under that power, they have glorious visions of the Eternal world.
息子のうちのふたりには、しばしば神の力が臨む。その力に打ち倒されているあいだ、ふたりは永遠の世界の栄光に満ちた幻を見る。
One son was healed of a swelling in the head.

The power of God comes upon them also in their own home; the Spirit of God speaks through them in other tongues, and they have visions.
自分たちの家でも、神の力がその人々に臨む。神の御霊が彼らを通して異言で語られ、彼らは幻を見る。

One woman had a tumor in her face and jaw for many years, was in an awful state, could hardly take any nourishment.
ある女は、何年ものあいだ顔とあごに腫瘍があった。ひどい状態で、ほとんど何も食べられなかった。
She came back and testified that it had all disappeared and the side of her face was straight like the other.
彼女はまたやって来て、腫瘍はすっかり消え、顔のその側ももう一方の側と同じようにまっすぐになった、と証しをした。

A man brought his wife on the way home to die, given up by all the doctors.
ある男が、医者たちみなに見放され、死ぬために家に帰る途中の妻を連れて来た。
She had cancer of the mouth, and she was saved and healed.
妻は口の癌を患っていたが、救われ、いやされた。
At once the swelling all went down and the soreness went, and she had the perfect use of her mouth.
たちまち腫れはすっかり引き、痛みも去って、口を完全に使えるようになった。
She came back the next day to testify that she was healed.
彼女は翌日またやって来て、いやされたと証しをした。

DALLAS MEETING

REPORT BY THE PASTOR, ELDER F. F. BOSWORTH

Having, during the past five years, read and re-read volumes 1 and 4 of "The Life, Work and Experience of Mrs. M. B. Woodworth-Etter," recording the wonderful Acts of the Holy Ghost in her evangelistic meetings during the past thirty-five years, we became very anxious for her to come and help us in the work at Dallas, Texas, where for many months we had been conducting Pentecostal meetings every night, during which time there had been a continual revival, many being saved, healed and baptized in the Holy Ghost according to Acts 2:4.

Feeling that it was God's plan for her to come, we wrote her concerning it, and in April, 1912, I visited her meeting, then being conducted in Indianapolis, Ind. During the three days' visit I witnessed some wonderful healing by the power of God, and met personally many who were wonderfully healed by the laying on of hands. Brother and Sister Etter also felt that God was leading to Dallas, so they came and began labor with us July 25th, 1912.

Throngs packed the large tent from the first, two or three thousand often standing around the tent. Great numbers of the sick and afflicted came, and were brought, on cots and in different ways, reminding us of the scenes described in the Gospels, when our Lord was upon earth.

Night after night, as soon as the invitation was given, all the available space around the fifty foot altar would be filled with so many suffering with diseases and afflictions and others seeking salvation and the Baptism in the Holy Ghost, that it was difficult to get in and out among the seekers. For several weeks there have been too many to be prayed for, for healing, and some each night have had to go away disappointed, and come again.

There were so many sick, that there was always a rush for the altar as soon as the invitation was given. Many have been wonderfully healed before the service began, and sometimes there has been no preaching--nothing but the altar service.

A hundred seekers often come to the altar at one time. Many have been saved and healed at the same time and have got up shouting and clapping their hands. Many who were not saved came to be healed. After listening to the Gospel they have realized that their souls were in a worse condition than their bodies and have dropped the matter of being healed until they made their peace with God.

The news of victory went out, and hundreds have already come from all over the United States, many bringing their sick hundreds of miles, some on cots in the baggage cars. Twenty states have been represented in a single service, coming on purpose to attend the meetings. Many ministers have come and gone and have received greater faith and power for the service of God. Many members of churches have come for healing and God has shown them that they had never been born of the Spirit.

In almost every service for nearly three months, the slaying power so common in the day of Finney, Jonathan Edwards, etc., has been manifested, often "The slain of the Lord" covering nearly all the altar space in the front of the tent. Many most wonderful visions and revelations, throwing light on the coming of the Lord, etc., have been given to those thus slain by the power of God. Many have seen balls of fire and lights in and around the tent (Acts 2, 3 and 26, 13). Some of these have been sinners. Jesus has appeared to many in the meetings, and many have at different times seen a great host of angels just above the audience.

In one service, several at the same time saw and heard the awful explosion of the mine near Constantinople, and screamed out at the same time; so this, and a number of awful battles in Turkey, and other calamities, have been seen and told in the meeting, before it appeared in the daily papers.

We praise God for bearing witness to the Gospel, not only with signs and wonders and divers miracles, but also with gifts of the Holy Ghost.

In accordance with Acts 19:12, handkerchiefs have been anointed and sent to the sick in all parts of the United States and Canada, and many have been wonderfully healed at the time the handkerchief was applied to their body. Others have been saved and healed while reading the reports of the meeting in the religious papers.

Besides great numbers being wonderfully saved and healed, about three hundred more have received the Baptism in the Holy Ghost during the last three months, speaking in other tongues as the Spirit gives utterance. Over one hundred of this number were from other states and towns. A number of these were ministers. We have just lately moved into our new large frame tabernacle, seating one thousand people, and Sunday hundreds were turned away, unable to get into the building. During the past few days people have come from the Atlantic to the Pacific to attend the meetings. We see no reason why still greater victories should not be given during the months to come. There has been a continuous revival here ever since February 2nd, 1911.

I have heard many preachers who could very eloquently point out what Christianity should be, and the wonderful possibilities of faith, but I know of none who, during the whole period of their ministry have had the faith to so wonderfully demonstrate the truth of Christ's declaration that "He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also," thus, "By manifestation of the truth commending herself to every man's conscience in the sight of God."

Although the revival here has continued every night for many months, the interest and power continues. Last night, hundreds were turned away, unable to get into the new tabernacle, which seats a thousand people.

Sister Etter is always seeking, and is continually urging the saints to seek more of God. Concerning the doctrine which she preaches, nothing needs to be said, for, as it was in the first century, God Himself bears witness "Both with signs and wonders and divers miracles and gifts of the Holy Ghost."

Joyfully expecting to meet the readers of this book in the clouds, when mortality shall put on immortality, and we shall be changed in the twinkling of an eye, and in our glorified bodies shall be forever with the Lord and with the redeemed, I am,

Your brother in Christ,

F. F. BOSWORTH.

REPORT OF EVANGELIST

In January, 1904, I heard that Mrs. M. B. Woodworth-Etter and her husband were going to hold Divine Healing meetings in St. Louis, and I went to their first meeting with a friend.

The mode of conducting the meeting, and some of the doctrines taught, were entirely new to me, but I knew at first sight that it was all of God, and that they were servants of God.

I helped in the meetings, two meetings each day, for more than six months. I saw miracles of healing; many prostrated under the mighty outpourings of God's Spirit from heaven; saw them lay with the light of Heaven on their faces, happier and more divine looking than anyone that I had ever seen. I saw them rise up after awhile, with their faces shining, full of praises to God, and heard them tell of the glories of the eternal world which they had just seen. I saw the visible manifestation of God's presence in many supernatural ways constantly.

The first time that I ever heard the Spirit speak in other tongues was in a meeting she was holding in Moline. A sister who had been used of God to do important evangelistic work said, referring to the tongues, she would believe after she had heard. Sister Etter told her she would be speaking in other tongues that very day, and in the afternoon the Spirit came on her in the meeting, and she commenced to sing in other tongues, with a very powerful voice. The singing was solemn, sad, and impressive. I never heard anything more so; it reminded me of the lamentation of the women at the Cross of Jesus, and of Jesus weeping over Jerusalem. We afterwards learned there was an earthquake taking place at that very time at Kingston, Jamaica, which the Lord showed to another sister in the meeting.

From there I went to Muscatine; found there an assembly which had been meeting ever since Sister Etter had held her last meetings in that city. Brothers and sisters were there who had been healed at that time, and they had been well ever since; and others who had through her teaching been firmly established in the deep things of God. I told them of how God was pouring out His Spirit elsewhere and speaking through some in other tongues and exhorted and taught them.

A short time after the Spirit came on an aged brother, and he began to speak in other tongues, this made the others hungry, and most of them commenced to seek for the same manifestation, and all received after various intervals, except one or two.

The Spirit did not speak through me till October, 1908, when He spake for about an hour and a half, and sang two songs through me. All glory and praise to God forever.

The Lord always backs up her preaching with the visible signs of His presence, which shows that Jesus is working with her, confirming the preaching with the signs accompanying and following.

She has the "power" of the Holy Ghost, or the gift of the Holy Ghost by the laying of hands and prayer; so often I have seen it especially at St. Louis, Indianapolis, and Dallas. Just lately out of ten whom she prayed for in succession the Spirit came on them in such power that they all fell prostrate and lay around the altar.

Of Brother Etter, I have seldom met a brother so entirely free from insincerity at all times. He speaks the truth in his heart, without guile or hypocrisy, and is ever ready to ratify and endorse his wife's preaching; he is equipped in every way for the work which God has called him to.

I know of no one God has so highly honored. I look to see her occupy a-high position in the Kingdom of our Lord in Heaven, and I have lived in their home for months at a time.

While I was in the work in England I got so hungry to be in their meetings again, that having received an invitation from them to come and again help in the meetings, I came clear across the Atlantic ocean direct to their meetings, and have been working with them since.

Yours, trusting alone in the Atoning Blood and Merits of Jesus, EVANGELIST.

W. J. MORTLOCK, England.
