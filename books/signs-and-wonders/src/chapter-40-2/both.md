# 第四十章　一九一三年のコネチカット州ロングヒルの野営集会（その二）
CHAPTER XL
CAMP-MEETING AT LONG HILL, CONN., 1913

## 百五十人がいやされる
ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY HEALED

（ここには、前の節に続いて、A・W・オットーの報告が載っていて、集会で見たいやしを伝えているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）
// ここから署名「A. W. OTTO, Editor and Publisher,」「Ottawa, Canada.」までは、前の節から続く A. W. Otto の報告。没年が確かめられないので訳さない。

During the two weeks I remained on the grounds, I believe not less than one hundred and fifty people were healed. We saw just as wonderful cases of healing take place as were ever wrought by the apostles. The person used of the Lord to perform these wonderful miracles was a woman named Mrs. Woodworth-Etter.

I can say that I have heard from her lips a stream of the most precious truths I ever listened to anywhere, or from any one, and I never heard any one that tried harder to exalt and honor the blessed Lord Jesus Christ. She is a humble, unassuming child of God, who is faithfully seeking to carry out the wonderful commission which He in His grace has committed to her hands; and the leading part of her work is the healing of the sick, and all who were oppressed ofthe devil.

The meetings usually began with the singing of several precious hymns, followed by several earnest prayers, which was succeeded by an address which was intended to stir up the faith of those who were seeking healing for their diseases. When an invitation was given, the sick and suffering would rush forward, that is, those who were able to come of their own accord, while those who were not able to walk, would be carried either on cots or chairs. At times the rush to get on the platform was so great that the sick had to be kept back by force, for they would have been "swallowed up," so to speak, the dear sister who was working with other sufferers so faithfully, that one often wondered how she could stand the terrible strain, indeed, she never could have done it, if not kept up by the mighty power of God, for she is now in her 69th year.

I saw people suffering from almost every kind of disease known to the medical profession, come on the platform, and in less than five minutes I saw these same people spring to their feet, and rush back and forth on the platform, which was about fifty feet long, shouting and leaping and praising God, and this often had the effect of setting us all leaping and shouting the praises of the Lord. If any one says this was all excitement of the flesh, let them read Acts III Ch. and tell me if it was mere excitement which made the lame man (whom Peter and John healed) rush into the temple, leaping and shouting the praises of the Lord.

I thought I was going to keep perfectly cool, but I soon found out that this was impossible. The one who could stand three feet from a man or woman who was suffering terribly, for example, with appendicitis, and in a few minutes see that same person spring to their feet and rush back and forth before that vast audience, leaping and shouting the praises of the Lord for the wonderful deliverance which He had so suddenly given them, without being moved, must certainly be in a strange state of soul.

I am not ashamed to confess that I often literally danced for joy, and shouted to the Lord as loud as any one on the platform, when I saw the Lord stretch forth His strong hand and rescue some dear suffering child of His from the power of the great enemy and set them free.

The following are a few of the cases which were healed right before my eyes. I talked with those people afterwards, and also heard them frequently giving their testimony before the assembled multitude.

The first case was that of a dear brother who went to the camp-meeting with me from Ottawa, Canada. He was suffering terribly from kidney disease, and lately several times nearly succumbed to the violent attacks of the disease. He was the first one Mrs. Etter prayed for, and laid her hands on, rebuking the demons who were afflicting him in the Name of the Lord Jesus, commanding them to depart, and, through faith in the Lord Jesus Christ, he claimed he had complete deliverance.

The next case I will mention was that of a man who roomed in the same house as myself. He was affected with some kind of heart disease, which to a large extent hindered the use of his arms and legs. He could not walk in a straight line nor was he sure of his footing, and when he fell down, which he did occasionally he was unable to rise again without assistance. His mind was affected and he was in a sad condition. He was instantly healed and became as active on his feet as it was possible for a man to be of his weight, for he was a very heavy, fat man.

A lady who was not only the wife of a physician, but who also has a son in the same profession, came to the grounds so sorely afflicted with appendicitis that she was almost in a dying condition. Her son and other doctors had insisted on her going to the hospital to undergo an operation, and if I remember rightly, had kept her from every kind of food for forty-eight hours to prepare her for the operation. But she refused to have the knife used on her, and in spite of their protestations, she came to the Camp-Grounds. I saw her when she arrived here. She came just as the meeting was about to close and after some pleadings with Mrs. Etter to pray for her, as Sister Etter was nearly exhausted, she laid hands on her and in the Name of the Lord Jesus Christ commanded the demons that were oppressing her to depart. This lady was a very intelligent person and talked very fluently, and she rushed up and down the platform almost beside herself with joy, shouting, and leaping, and praising the Lord in such a wonderful way that it set all the people shouting and praising the Lord. She also pounded on her body over the appendix with her clenched fist, until it almost sounded like a drum, to show that all trace of the disease had departed. I never witnessed anything like it and never expect to again.

Next day a lady came on the grounds with the strangest disease I ever heard of. She could not allow any one to touch her for fear they had previously handled something sweet and because of this terrible mania she could not touch a door knob to open a door. She also wore a pair of elbow length gloves to protect her hands and arms from anything sweet, for five years. Her hands and arms were as red as a piece of raw beefsteak from the constant scrubbing to keep them free of sweets and if her husband kissed her she would literally scour her face to get the sweetness off. I saw her as they gathered around the table and the sugar was just in front of her, and it was like snakes to her. She turned away and began to weep.

No one could live with her, so her husband was forced to leave her. I saw her prayed for, and in two or three minutes completely delivered from the grasp of the enemy, and I heard her shouting and praising the Lord. A brother stepped up and asked her to shake hands with him, and this she readily did before the whole congregation, with her gloves off, a thing she would not have been able to do for many years previous to this. Her hands almost immediately regained their natural color, and it was a wonderful joy to see her going about among the people every day rejoicing in her great deliverance. She wrote to her husband and told him the precious news, and I heard his answer read in which he expressed his great joy to learn that his dear wife had been delivered from the power of the enemy. (Weeks afterward her testimony appeared in the paper showing that she was still healed.)

Another young girl was afflicted with curvature of the spine. She saw so many rushing forward to be healed, that she despaired of ever being able to get on the platform, so she took her case to the Lord in her seat. By this time the power was so great in the meeting that some were not only getting their baptism in the Holy Ghost in their seats, but also getting healed, and this was the case with this young girl. She was instantly healed and also received her baptism, and I heard her giving her testimony to that effect. She not only spoke in an unknown tongue for two and one-half hours, but also interpreted what she said. If this is not a miracle, there never was one.

Her companion was brought on the platform so very sick that she was almost fainting. She had severe stomach trouble. She was instantly healed, and was so filled with the Holy Ghost while giving her testimony that she fell into the arms of those standing nearest her, and had to be laid down, where she remained under the power for a long time.

All these cases took place, and a hundred more like them, right under my own eyes, and I cannot be in the least mistaken. I saw many cases of cancer, appendicitis, rheumatism, nervous affections of various kinds, paralysis, lameness, etc., healed in a wonderful way, but space forbids that I give any more in this number, but hope to be able to do so in the next, if the Lord wills. I myself felt the healing touch in a wonderful way, and know whereof I speak. Praise the Lord. Praise the Lord forever!

No brethren, the days of miracles have not passed. The Lord's promise in his commission in Mark 16.--15 to 18, is just as true today as it was when he uttered these words, and although so many are rejecting it as not being for us now, to those who do believe and accept his word as meaning them, He is even now showing forth His wonderful power, not only to save souls, and also to heal their diseases, but to baptize them in the Holy Ghost.

A. W. OTTO, Editor and Publisher,

Ottawa, Canada.

## イエスの名によって行われた驚くべき奇跡
WONDERFUL MIRACLES WROUGHT IN JESUS' NAME (An Open Letter to the Evangel Readers by an Eye Witness.

World-Wide Camp-Meeting, Long Hill, Conn., June 19, 1913.)
（目撃者が『エバンジェル』誌の読者に送る公開書簡。一九一三年六月十九日、コネチカット州ロングヒルの世界野営集会にて）

I want to give you a slight glimpse of what God is doing in the World-Wide Camp-Meeting at Long Hill, Conn. I was never privileged to be where the power of God was so manifestly poured out; many baptized in the Holy Ghost; and many speaking in tongues; some powerful cases of salvation to sinners and reclamation of backsliders.
コネチカット州ロングヒルの世界野営集会で神がなさっていることの一端を、みなさんに伝えたい。神の力がこれほどはっきりと注がれている所に居合わせたことは、これまでなかった。多くの人が聖霊のバプテスマを受け、多くの人が異言を語っている。罪人が救われ、背信者が立ち返る力強い例もいくつかある。

God is working on all lines, but particularly in the healing of the sick. The first two weeks there were three hundred and fifty prayed for. A missionary carefully tabulated the healings after investigation, and the following list is a part of that table: Neuralgia, neurasthenia, scrofula, lameness, appendicitis, mysophobia, blindness, deafness, dropsy, tumors, goitre, hernia, grippe, catarrh, kidney trouble, heart disease, bronchial affections, bowel ailments, liver complaint, displaced vertebrae, curvature of the spine.
神はあらゆる面で働いておられるが、とくに病人のいやしにおいて働いておられる。最初の二週間で、三百五十人が祈ってもらった。ひとりの宣教師が、調べたうえで、いやしを丹念に表にまとめた。次に挙げるのは、その表の一部である。神経痛、神経衰弱、るいれき、歩行の障害、虫垂炎、不潔恐怖症、失明、難聴、水腫、腫瘍、甲状腺腫、脱腸、流行性感冒、カタル、腎臓病、心臓病、気管支の病、腸の病、肝臓病、脊椎のずれ、脊椎の湾曲。

It is difficult to keep track of the healings they multiply so rapidly. We are in the third week of the meeting and the power of God is increasing all the time. There is a very large attendance of Pentecostal workers, and great unity of the Spirit. A marked feature of the meeting is the strong, direct teaching of the Word, the absence of fanaticism and the repudiation of errors which here and there have crept into Pentecostal work. God Himself is steadying the ark. Bless His precious Name!
いやしがあまりに速く増えていくので、とても数えきれない。集会は三週目に入り、神の力はますます増し加わっている。ペンテコステの働き人たちが大勢集まり、御霊による深い一致がある。この集会のきわだった特色は、みことばの力強くまっすぐな教えである。熱狂がなく、ペンテコステの働きにあちこちで入り込んだ誤りが退けられている。神ご自身が、神の箱を支えておられるのである。尊い御名をほめたたえよ。

The unity of the Spirit in which He holds us all, is most hallowed and deepened from hour to hour. The praises He pours into our hearts and puts through our lips are mighty, and on this volume of praise the Holy Spirit moves out to do His work. The high praises of God, a TWO-EDGED SWORD, Ps. 149:6. We are proving that the walls of the city (Spiritual Zion) are salvation and her gates praise. Isa. 60:18. We enter in by praise. Ps. 100:4. The enemy falls before the voice of the Holy Ghost praise. II. Chron. 20.21-23.
神は私たちみなを御霊の一致のうちに保っておられ、その一致は時を追うごとに聖められ、深められている。神が私たちの心に注いでくださり、唇から出させてくださる賛美は力強い。そして、この賛美の高まりに乗って、聖霊は出て行かれ、そのみわざをなさる。神をあがめる歌は、もろ刃のつるぎである（詩篇一四九・六）。町（霊のシオン）の城壁は救いであり、その門は誉れであることを、私たちは身をもって確かめている（イザヤ六〇・一八）。私たちは賛美によって入る（詩篇一〇〇・四）。敵は、聖霊による賛美の声の前に倒れる（歴代下二〇・二一〜二三）。

One dear drunken sinner was brought to meeting in an automobile. After prayer he fell off into a drunken sleep under the altar. When he had slept off the effects of liquor he arose and began to call on God. He was converted, baptized with the Holy Ghost and healed that night. The next night he brought his wife who was saved, and then followed their son's salvation.
酔いつぶれたひとりの哀れな罪人が、自動車で集会に連れて来られた。祈りの後、彼は祭壇の下で酔って眠り込んでしまった。酔いがさめると、彼は起き上がって神を呼び求めはじめた。その夜、彼は回心し、聖霊のバプテスマを受け、いやされた。次の夜、彼は妻を連れて来て、妻も救われた。それから息子も救われた。

A colored girl, the wall of whose abdomen burst thirteen years ago, after an operation, and the contents of large intestines came constantly through the sides, had been in the hospital for a year and had several operations--now healed by the power of God.
ひとりの黒人の娘がいた。十三年前、手術の後に腹壁が破れ、それ以来、大腸の中身が絶えず脇から出てくるようになった。彼女は一年間入院し、何度も手術を受けていた。その娘が今、神の力によっていやされた。

A goitre of twenty-five years standing went down somewhat with the laying on of hands and the healing is now accomplished Mrs. Mead, fifteen years a cripple, rheumatism in one knee, varicose veins in other leg, unable to bend knees for fifteen years, received immediate deliverance when prayed for.
二十五年来の甲状腺腫が、手を置かれるとともにいくらか小さくなり、今ではすっかりいやされている。ミード夫人は、十五年間歩くのが不自由だった。片方の膝はリウマチで、もう片方の脚には静脈瘤があり、十五年間膝を曲げることができなかった。祈ってもらうと、夫人はたちまち解放された。

One from Ellendale, Ont., blind seven years from accident, one eye destroyed, the other blind, total blindness for two years, saw the sun for the first time after his baptism of the Holy Ghost. The baptism was accompanied by two large balls of fire and a small one, which entered the eye, from which he has since seen the sun, and more and more of other objects daily. In this case the healing is gradual, also with a Ottawa brother who had catarrh for thirty-eight years, derangement of intestines for fourteen years and kidney trouble for four years.
オンタリオ州エレンデールから来たひとりは、事故のために七年間目が見えなかった。片方の目はつぶれ、もう片方も見えず、この二年は完全に失明していた。その人は聖霊のバプテスマを受けた後、初めて太陽を見た。そのバプテスマには、二つの大きな火の玉と一つの小さな火の玉が伴い、その小さな火の玉が目に入った。それ以来、彼は太陽を見ることができ、ほかの物も日ごとによく見えるようになっている。この人の場合、いやしは徐々に進んでいる。オタワのある兄弟もそうである。彼は三十八年間カタルを、十四年間腸の障害を、四年間腎臓病を患っていた。

I have given so much prominence in this letter to the healing work of God hoping it will encourage poor sufferers everywhere to trust their God for healing. He is no respecter of persons and says, "According to your faith be it unto you." "I am the Lord that healeth thee."
この手紙では、神のいやしの働きをこれほど大きく取り上げた。各地の哀れな病む人々が励まされ、いやしを神に信頼するようになることを願うからである。神は人をかたよりみることをなさらず、こう言われる。『あなたがたの信仰どおり、あなたがたの身になるように』（マタイ九・二九）。『わたしは主であって、あなたをいやすものである』（出エジプト一五・二六）。

To my own soul the most wonderful feature of these meetings, is the glorious Shekinah of God's presence. Often He comes in the hallelujah choir and with it a heavenly radiance on every Christian face which is truly indescribable, but a rich commentary of "He shall change the body of our humiliation and make it like unto His glorious body." (Phil. IV. R. V.)
私自身の魂にとって、この集会の最も驚くべき特色は、神の臨在を示す栄光の輝き（シェキナ）である。神はしばしばハレルヤの聖歌隊のうちに来られる。それとともに、天の輝きがすべてのクリスチャンの顔に宿る。それはまことに言い表しようのないものだが、次の聖句の豊かな注解となっている。『わたしたちの卑しいからだを、ご自身の栄光のからだと同じかたちに変えて下さるであろう』（ピリピ三・二一）。

It is truly remarkable how God sustains His honored servant, our beloved sister Etter who has charge of this camp. At sixty-nine years of age God daily reinforces her strength in the use of the gift of healing, upholding her in arduous labors that, unsustained by God, the vigor of young manhood could not endure.
神がご自分の尊い僕を支えておられるさまは、まことに驚くべきものである。その僕とは、この野営集会を任されている、私たちの愛するエター姉妹である。六十九歳の姉妹に、神は日ごとに力を加えてくださり、いやしの賜物を用いさせておられる。神が支えてくださらなければ、若い男の活力でも耐えられないような重い働きの中で、神は姉妹を支えておられるのである。

Later--There came into this morning's meeting an unconverted mother, bringing her unconverted daughter of seventeen years. The girl had come three hundred and fifty miles for blindness to be healed. She having been stone blind since the age of two years. While Sister Etter's hands were on her in prayer, sight was given, and 'twas most touching to see the mother and daughter weepingly embrace and gaze with joy upon one another. Now, for the first time that she could remember, the girl saw her mother's face.
追記　今朝の集会に、回心していないひとりの母親が、回心していない十七歳の娘を連れて来た。娘は目をいやしてもらうために、三百五十マイルの道をやって来たのである。二歳のときから、まったく目が見えなかった。エター姉妹が手を置いて祈っていると、視力が与えられた。母と娘が泣きながら抱き合い、喜びのうちに互いを見つめ合う姿は、実に胸を打つものだった。娘は覚えているかぎり初めて、母の顔を見たのである。

We are praising God that in so many instances the unconverted come to trust Jesus for healing. It would seem that they sometimes trust more than His saved people! Oh, the damnable shortage of faith among "His Own." "He came unto His own and they received Him not." "When the Son of Man cometh shall He find faith upon the earth?"
回心していない人々が、これほど多くの場合に、いやしを求めてイエスに信頼するようになったことを、私たちは神をほめたたえている。ときには、救われた神の民よりも、彼らのほうがよく信頼しているように見える。ああ、『自分の民』のあいだに信仰が足りないとは、なんと忌まわしいことだろう。『彼は自分のところにきたのに、自分の民は彼を受けいれなかった』（ヨハネ一・一一）。『人の子が来るとき、地上に信仰が見られるであろうか』（ルカ一八・八）。

Still later--Another notable healing! A father came from a distance bringing his deaf and dumb boy--so born. He had been in an institution for education, and had become so prejudiced and so filled with hatred that when the father brought him to the grounds he was like a wild animal and had to be restrained by the father from breaking away and running to the woods. As soon as hands were laid on him in the name of Jesus, the demons came out, his face became sweet and lovely, the hearing came, and he is learning to talk. Glory to God! The Spiritual tide is rising every hour.
さらに追記　またも目ざましいいやしだ！　ひとりの父親が、生まれつき耳が聞こえず口のきけない息子を、遠くから連れて来た。息子は教育のための施設に入っていたが、ひどく偏見にとらわれ、憎しみに満ちるようになっていた。父親が会場に連れて来たときには、野獣のようだった。父親は、息子が振りほどいて森へ逃げて行かないよう、押さえていなければならなかった。イエスの名によって手が置かれるとすぐ、悪霊どもが出て行った。息子の顔はやさしく愛らしくなり、耳が聞こえるようになった。そして今、話すことを学んでいる。神に栄光あれ！　霊の潮は刻一刻と満ちてきている。

June 27--I must add an appendix to my camp-meeting letter, for God has broken forth in a most astonishing manner in the camp. Yesterday morning the presence and power of God came suddenly upon us; while in worship and adoration of Him one fell to the floor under the "weight of glory." Then a clergyman came forth and gave a powerful message in tongues; he seemed not quite finished when he, too, was prostrated.
六月二十七日　この野営集会の手紙に、付け加えなければならないことがある。神が野営地で、まことに驚くべきしかたで現れてくださったからである。昨日の朝、神の臨在と力が突然私たちに臨んだ。神を礼拝し、あがめていると、ひとりが『重い栄光』（第二コリント四・一七）の下に床に倒れた。それから、ひとりの牧師が進み出て、異言で力強い言葉を語った。まだ語り終えていないように見えたが、彼もまた倒れ伏した。

Then with a kind of rushing sound came the "power" and all over the house people fell under it. I counted fourteen on the platform and all around it--impossible to say how many fell all over the place, the slain of the Lord were many. There followed a scene that beggars all description, as under the increasing light and power of God, His people worshipped Him, sometimes in the cooing of the Dove--the Holy Dove of God--the Holy Ghost bringing forth from the innermost depths of the being sweet love notes to Jesus.
すると、激しい風のような音とともに「力」が臨み、会場じゅうで人々がその下に倒れた。壇の上とその周りで、私は十四人を数えた。会場全体でどれほどの人が倒れたかは、とても言えない。主に打ち倒される者は多かった。続いて、どんな言葉でも描き尽くせない光景が起こった。神の光と力が増していく中で、神の民は神を礼拝した。ときには、鳩のくうくうと鳴く声で礼拝した。その鳩とは神の聖なる鳩、聖霊であり、存在のいちばん奥深くから、イエスへの甘い愛の調べを引き出してくださったのである。

The voices were many, for all in the tent seemed worshipping; the sound was one, the comingled sound of many waters. No drilled choir could have kept in.such harmony and unity, with sweetest melody. The bandmaster was evidently the Holy Ghost. He can render music without rehearsals on a company of yielded instruments. . Glory! The "Hallelujah Chorus,". was varied by most solemn, searching, loving messages in tongues, with interpretations.
声は多かった。天幕の中のすべての人が礼拝しているようだったからである。しかし音は一つだった。大水のとどろきが一つに混じり合った音である。どんなに訓練された聖歌隊でも、これほどの調和と一致を、これほど甘い旋律で保つことはできなかっただろう。指揮者が聖霊であることは明らかだった。聖霊は、明け渡された楽器の一団で、練習なしに音楽を奏でることがおできになる。栄光あれ！　「ハレルヤ・コーラス」の合間には、きわめて厳かで、心を探り、愛に満ちた異言の言葉が、解き明かしとともに語られた。

Meantime many were having visions, some seeing Jesus riding on the white horse of power, some seeing troops of angels hovering over us; one saw blood everywhere, and the red horse of war striding on. Crowds of sinners stood on the outskirt of the tent looking on; most respectful and solemn attention was given, and some of the messages were for them, but most were for the saints. Several came through at this time into their baptism.
そのあいだに、多くの人が幻を見ていた。イエスが力の白い馬に乗っておられるのを見た人々や、御使の大群が私たちの上を舞っているのを見た人々がいた。ひとりは、至る所に血があり、戦いの赤い馬が大またに進んで行くのを見た。罪人たちの群れが天幕の外側に立って、見守っていた。彼らはきわめて敬意をもって、厳粛に耳を傾けていた。言葉のいくつかは彼らに向けられていたが、ほとんどは聖徒たちに向けられたものだった。このとき、何人かが聖霊のバプテスマを受けるに至った。

This meeting began at 9 A. M., and closed at 1 P. M. We reassembled at 3 P. M., when the power immediately came upon all more strongly than in the morning. Two gray-headed sinners pushed their way through the crowd and surrendered to Jesus. One business man said, "I have locked up my place of business and came a sin-sick soul. For days I have watched these meetings, seen the healings and the mighty power and said, 'If this be the power of God, I dare not say a word against it.' Now I know it is the power of God." He and the other white-haired man were both gloriously saved, coming out into the light almost immediately; they then began to push on for their baptism.
この集会は午前九時に始まり、午後一時に終わった。午後三時に再び集まると、たちまち朝よりも強く、力がすべての人に臨んだ。白髪の罪人がふたり、群衆をかき分けて進み出て、イエスに身をゆだねた。そのひとりは実業家で、こう言った。「私は店を閉めて、罪に病んだ魂として来ました。何日も、この集会を見守ってきました。いやしと大いなる力を見て、『もしこれが神の力なら、それに反対することは一言も言えない』と言ったのです。今は、これが神の力だと知っています」彼ももうひとりの白髪の男も、ふたりとも輝かしく救われ、ほとんどすぐに光の中へ出て来た。それから彼らは、バプテスマを求めて進みはじめた。

Truly, Apostolic Days are upon us! Many hymns and songs of worship were given in tongues. People lying on their backs with closed eyes at a distance from one another would sing these hymns in the same tongue in perfect unison. These duets in tongues were often followed by the song in interpretation. Many of those who were healed were leaping and dancing under the power, reminding one of the scene in Jerusalem when the onlookers said, "They are filled with new wine." Yes, "This was that spoken by the prophet Joel' of "the last days."
まことに、使徒の時代が私たちに臨んでいる。多くの賛美歌と礼拝の歌が、異言で与えられた。互いに離れた所で、目を閉じてあおむけに横たわっている人々が、同じ異言でこれらの賛美歌を完全にそろって歌うのである。この異言の二重唱には、しばしば解き明かしの歌が続いた。いやされた人の多くは、力の下で跳びはね、踊っていた。それは、エルサレムでの光景を思い起こさせた。そのとき見ていた人々は、『あの人たちは新しい酒で酔っているのだ』（使徒二・一三）と言ったのである。そうだ、『これは預言者ヨエルが預言していたこと』（使徒二・一六）であり、それは『終りの時』（使徒二・一七）についての預言である。

Truly, we are in the last of the last days. The shortness of the time until Jesus comes was emphasized again by the Spirit-filled saints and most tenderly, pleadingly, did the Heavenly Dove, the Holy Ghost, speak in many languages for us to get oil and prepare for the Heavenly Bridegroom. These messages were interpreted for us by others under the power. Surely we shall be without excuse if we are found without "oil in our vessels with our lamps" in these last days!
まことに、私たちは終わりの時の、そのまた終わりにいる。イエスが来られるまでの時が短いことが、御霊に満たされた聖徒たちによって再び強調された。そして天の鳩である聖霊が、多くの言葉で、実にやさしく、嘆願するように語られた。油を手に入れ、天の花婿を迎える備えをせよ、と。これらの言葉は、力の下にある別の人々によって解き明かされた。この終わりの時に、『自分たちのあかりと一緒に、入れものの中に油を』（マタイ二五・四）用意していないなら、私たちには弁解の余地がない。

This meeting, of which I can give you no idea, closed about six o'clock P. M. We soon reassembled and the Spirit held us in His power until 11 P. M., the awe deepening upon us. The majesty and glory of God became so great we could hardly breathe. No one looked to his neighbor, but all felt as though in the immediate presence of God.
この集会のことは、とても言い表すことができない。集会は午後六時ごろに終わった。私たちはすぐに再び集まり、御霊は午後十一時まで私たちをその力のうちにとどめられた。畏れが私たちの上にますます深まっていった。神の威光と栄光があまりに大きく、私たちはほとんど息もできなかった。だれも隣の人を見なかった。みな、神のすぐ御前にいるように感じていた。

An immense crowd of the unsaved had gathered and stood many feet deep all around the densely packed tent. Pungent, tender and solemn were the messages given them; in sacred pantomime (Ezekiel 4:1-8! 12:3-7, etc.,) was enacted the crucifixion of our Lord for them, His coming crowned to receive the lost ones who would let Him save them now. No altar service was called and the meeting closed in a deathlike stillness. We wended our way home caring to see and speak to no one; the sinners likewise; not a sound was heard as they trooped down the hills to the cars. As we came out of the tent we found "the slain of the Lord" lying all around.
救われていない人々の大群衆が集まり、ぎっしり詰まった天幕の周りを、何重にも取り囲んで立っていた。その人々に与えられた言葉は、鋭く、やさしく、厳粛だった。聖なる無言劇によって（エゼキエル四・一〜八、一二・三〜七など）、彼らのために私たちの主の十字架が演じられた。また、今、主に救っていただこうとする失われた者たちを迎えるために、主が冠をかぶって来られることも演じられた。祭壇への招きはなく、集会は死のような静けさのうちに終わった。私たちは、だれにも会いたくなく、話もしたくない思いで、家路をたどった。罪人たちも同じだった。彼らが群れをなして丘を下り、路面電車へ向かうあいだ、物音一つ聞こえなかった。天幕から出ると、『主に打ち倒される者』（イザヤ六六・一六）が至る所に横たわっていた。

"We have not followed cunningly devised fables when we made known unto you the power and coming of the Lord Jesus Christ, but were eye-witnesses of His majesty." His honor and glory, as we have been with Him in this holy mount.
『わたしたちの主イエス・キリストの力と来臨とを、あなたがたに知らせた時、わたしたちは、巧みな作り話を用いることはしなかった。わたしたちが、そのご威光の目撃者なのだからである』（第二ペテロ一・一六）。この聖なる山で主と共にいたので、私たちは主のほまれと栄光の目撃者でもある。
