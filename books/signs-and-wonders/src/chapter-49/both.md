# 第四十九章　ペンシルベニア州フィラデルフィアの野営集会（一九一四年）
CHAPTER XLIX
CAMP MEETING AT PHILADELPHIA, PA., 1914

We went to Philadelphia, Pa., at the call of Brother Wm. Anderson and a few saints. There were many missions in the city, but not two united. Only a few were willing to stand with Brother Anderson, but the few put their whole energy with their money in the work. The Lord was in the move, the building went up. We found a nice tent at the end of the car line, with many small ones, and a fine tent for meals. Also, a nice home, well furnished, for myself and workers, with a sister there to cook and care for us. They gave us a warm welcome, and dear brother Anderson stood so nobly by us all through the meeting and helped to push the battle to the gate.
私たちは、ウィリアム・アンダーソン兄弟と数人の聖徒の招きで、ペンシルベニア州フィラデルフィアへ行った。町には多くの伝道所があったが、どの二つも互いに一致していなかった。アンダーソン兄弟と共に立とうとする者はわずかだった。しかしその少数の人々は、力のかぎりを尽くし、お金も注いで、この働きに当たった。主がこの動きの中におられ、建物はできあがった。行ってみると、路面電車の終点に立派な天幕が張られていて、小さな天幕もたくさんあり、食事のための立派な天幕もあった。また、私と働き人たちのために、家具のよく整った立派な家も用意されていた。そこではひとりの姉妹が料理をし、私たちの世話をしてくれた。彼らは私たちを温かく迎えてくれた。愛するアンダーソン兄弟は、集会のあいだずっと私たちを実に気高く支え、戦いを門まで追い返す助けをしてくれた。
// イザヤ二八・六 の言葉。

We would not permit any antagonistic spirit to come in; no doctrines aired, that would cause strife or division. The spirit of love was felt; they began to come from all the missions and felt that they were one in Christ Jesus. All felt their need of getting deeper in God. As the great crowds continued to increase, the presence of God was seen and felt by all. As the Word was preached with power, God demonstrated with mighty miracles, "Signs and Wonders," till the sinners trembled and cried out, "What shall I do to be saved?"
私たちは、敵対する霊が入り込むことを許さなかった。争いや分裂を引き起こすような教理は、持ち出させなかった。愛の霊が感じられた。人々はあらゆる伝道所から来はじめ、自分たちがキリスト・イエスにあって一つであると感じた。だれもが、神のうちにもっと深く入る必要を感じた。大群衆がますます増えていくにつれて、神の臨在がすべての人に見え、感じられた。みことばが力をもって宣べ伝えられると、神は力ある奇跡、すなわち「しるしと不思議」によって、それを目に見える形で示された。ついには罪人たちが震えて、『救われるために、何をすべきでしょうか』と叫んだ（使徒一六・三〇）。

Multitudes of men and women were healed and turned to the Lord. Many were baptized with the Holy Spirit, and gave strong messages in other tongues, as the Spirit gave utterance.
大勢の男女がいやされ、主に立ち返った。多くの人が聖霊のバプテスマを受け、御霊が語らせるままに、異言による力強い言葉を語った。

God wonderfully used Pastor Anderson, in tones of thunder, sounding like the voice of God. It was, for God said in the last days "with stammering lips and another tongue" will I speak to this people, but for all that some will not believe. (Isa. xxviii, 11,12.) Then the interpretation was given. Old men, women and children spake in languages, preaching and prophesying, singing new songs, the words of which had never before been heard; magnifying and praising the Lord. Many of all classes joined the Holy Dance, or joy dance, filled with the Resurrection Life and Lightness, dancing with closed eyes, faces bright and shining, sometimes playing with unseen instruments, singing and dancing to their own music.
神はアンダーソン牧師を驚くほど用いられた。彼は雷のような調子で、神の御声のように響く声で語った。実際、それは神の御声だった。神は、終わりの時には『異国のくちびると、異国の舌とをもって』この民に語る、それでもなお信じない者がいる、と言われたのである（イザヤ二八・一一〜一二）。続いて、解き明かしが与えられた。老いた男も女も子供も、いろいろな国の言葉で語り、説教し、預言し、新しい歌を歌った。その歌詞は、これまで一度も聞かれたことのないものだった。彼らは主をあがめ、ほめたたえた。あらゆる階層の多くの人々が、聖なる踊り、すなわち喜びの踊りに加わった。彼らは復活のいのちと軽やかさに満たされ、目を閉じて踊った。顔は明るく輝いていた。ときには目に見えない楽器を奏で、自分たちの音楽に合わせて歌い、踊った。
// 英文の出典は「Isa. xxviii, 11,12.」。「but for all that some will not believe」は第一コリント一四・二一 の言い方に近い。

Bright lights were at times seen over the pulpit and over various people. The Lord was using a brother in other tongues, prophesying and interpreting. A Methodist minister sitting in the congregation came forward and said that he saw an angel standing by the brother all the time while the brother was talking. He was very much affected and said he had to come forward and tell what he had seen. Nearly all classes were convinced and convicted. Policemen and street car conductors took a great interest. I do not know how many were saved and healed.
説教壇の上や、いろいろな人の上に、ときどき明るい光が見えた。主はひとりの兄弟を異言で用いておられ、彼は預言し、解き明かしていた。会衆の中に座っていたひとりのメソジストの牧師が、前に出て来て言った。その兄弟が語っているあいだずっと、ひとりの御使がそのそばに立っているのを見た、と。彼はたいへん心を動かされ、自分の見たことを前に出て語らずにはいられなかったと言った。ほとんどあらゆる階層の人々が納得し、罪を示された。警官や路面電車の車掌たちも、大きな関心を寄せた。どれほどの人が救われ、いやされたのか、私にはわからない。

Our last meeting was on Labor Day. We had a special meeting on that day, to pray for ministers, evangelists and workers for the baptism, fresh anointings and power for service; for the stirring up of gifts and new gifts to be imparted by the laying on of hands. We had three meetings. The large tent was crowded, people standing all around; though so crowded, we had the best of order. Everybody was interested and wanted to see and hear, or get some special blessing. The glory of the Lord was like a cloud, it swept over and over the place. We placed chairs on the platform; those who wanted special prayer and laying on of hands sat in the chairs. The power of God fell on everyone. They all got filled with resurrection life, leaped, danced and shouted praises to God. Most of them fell and lay in green pastures. Scores of men and women were lying like dead all around. Strong men and women were weeping, both in and outside of the tent.
最後の集会は労働者の日だった。その日、私たちは特別の集会を開き、牧師、伝道者、働き人たちのために祈った。彼らがバプテスマと新たな油注ぎと奉仕のための力を受けるように、また賜物が燃えたたせられ、手を置くことによって新しい賜物が授けられるように、と祈ったのである。集会は三度開いた。大天幕は人でいっぱいで、周りじゅうに人が立っていた。それほど混み合っていたが、秩序は申し分なく保たれた。だれもが関心を持ち、見たい、聞きたい、何か特別な祝福を受けたいと願っていた。主の栄光は雲のようで、その場所を幾度も幾度も吹き渡った。私たちは壇の上に椅子を並べた。特別な祈りと手を置くことを望む人々は、その椅子に座った。神の力がひとりひとりの上に下った。彼らはみな復活のいのちに満たされ、跳びはね、踊り、叫んで神をほめたたえた。そのほとんどが倒れて、緑の牧場に伏した。何十人もの男女が、周りじゅうに死んだように横たわっていた。たくましい男や女が、天幕の中でも外でも泣いていた。

While we were holding a meeting in Chicago some months later, a minister, speaking of this meeting in Philadelphia, said the last day--Labor Day--he counted one hundred and fifty that were struck down that day by the power of God. Surely the slain of the Lord were many. The work spread far and near. New missions sprung up as a result of this meeting in Philadelphia. Now, after nearly two years, I have a pressing call from the same minister to hold meetings. He has a strong assembly in another part of the city that came from that meeting. Praise God the work is going on.
数か月後、私たちがシカゴで集会を開いていたとき、ひとりの牧師がこのフィラデルフィアの集会のことを話した。最後の日、すなわち労働者の日に、神の力に打ち倒された人をその日だけで百五十人数えたという。まことに、主に打ち倒される者は多かった。働きは遠くにも近くにも広がった。このフィラデルフィアの集会の結果、新しい伝道所がいくつも起こった。二年近くたった今、私はその同じ牧師から、集会を開いてほしいという切なる招きを受けている。彼は町の別の地区に、力強い会衆を持っている。それはあの集会から生まれたものである。神をほめたたえよ。働きは続いている。

## 集会の報告
REPORT OF THE MEETING

（ここには、ウィリアム・アンダーソン牧師の報告が載っているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）

By the Pastor

The work that the Lord did here through Sister Etter was more than a natural man could understand. There were many wonderful works of healing wrought by the power of God. Many were saved from sin and others were baptized with the Holy Ghost, with the same outward sign as recorded in Acts 2-4 of speaking in other tongues as the Spirit of God gave utterance.

Two young girls got saved and baptized with the Holy Ghost, and God has since wonderfully manifested His power in their home; in so much that their four sisters and their father have since been saved, the four sisters and their mother having also received the Baptism of the Holy Ghost. Through meetings held in their home a number have since been saved and baptized. The meetings are still going on and God is still continuing to bless. Glory to His Holy Name.

Many people came to the camp meeting from other towns and cities, and were wonderfully blessed, several of them receiving the Baptism of the Holy Ghost, whom God has since used to be a blessing to many others.

Yours in the Master's service,

WM. ANDERSON.

## 会衆の報告　フィラデルフィアのペンテコステ野営集会
THE ASSEMBLY'S REPORT--PHILADELPHIA PENTECOSTAL CAMP MEETING

## 責任者　ウッドワース・エター夫人
MRS. M. B. WOODWORTH-ETTER IN CHARGE

The Pentecostal Camp Meeting opened on August 1, 1914, Sister M. B. Woodworth-Etter in charge.
ペンテコステ野営集会は、一九一四年八月一日に始まった。責任者はM・B・ウッドワース・エター姉妹である。

Many have been built up in Biblical Faith and Love in the Holy Spirit.
多くの人が、聖書の信仰と聖霊による愛のうちに築き上げられた。

The Gospel of Jesus was proclaimed, not only in word, but in demonstration and Power of the Holy Spirit. God has confirmed His Word with many signs, manifesting His Holy Presence in mighty power.
イエスの福音は、言葉だけでなく、聖霊の現れと力とによって宣べ伝えられた。神は多くのしるしをもってみことばを確かなものとされ、力強い力のうちに聖なる臨在を現された。

The outpouring of the Holy Spirit was marvelous to behold. How true the prophecy: "I will pour My Spirit upon all flesh." It has been very refreshing to the saints, strengthening their faith and begetting in their hearts a hunger for the deeper things of God, reclaiming backsliders and bringing repentance and salvation to sinners. Many have been baptized in the Holy Spirit, and were speaking with new tongues as the Spirit gave them utterance.
聖霊の注ぎは、目を見張るばかりだった。『わが霊をすべての肉なる者に注ぐ』という預言は、なんと真実なことだろう（ヨエル二・二八）。それは聖徒たちを大いに元気づけ、彼らの信仰を強め、神のより深いことへの渇きを彼らの心に生み出した。また、後戻りした者を立ち返らせ、罪人に悔い改めと救いをもたらした。多くの人が聖霊のバプテスマを受け、御霊が語らせるままに新しい言葉を語った。

A man came 200 miles to be healed of locomotor ataxia. He had to be helped to the camp by two men, but when the Lord touched him he threw away his cane and walked alone.
ひとりの男が、歩行運動失調症をいやしてもらうために、二百マイルの道のりをやって来た。彼はふたりの男に助けられて野営地まで来なければならなかったが、主が触れられると、杖を投げ捨て、ひとりで歩いた。

A man was perfectly healed from a rupture after wearing a truss for 20 years.
二十年間脱腸帯をつけていたひとりの男が、脱腸を全くいやされた。

The signers to the following testimonials, with grateful hearts to the Lord for the wonderful things He has done for them, gladly tell their experiences for the praise of His dear Name.
次の証しに名を記した人々は、主が自分たちにしてくださったすばらしいことのゆえに、主に感謝の心を抱いている。そして、愛する御名をほめたたえるために、喜んで自分の経験を語っている。

I praise God for His healing power. He healed me of a goitre which I have had all my life, born with it. When Sister Etter laid her hands on me I felt the healing power go through me and the goitre is disappearing rapidly. Praise God!
神のいやしの力のゆえに、神をほめたたえます。神は、生まれつき一生抱えてきた甲状腺腫を、いやしてくださいました。エター姉妹が私に手を置いてくださったとき、いやしの力が私の体を貫くのを感じました。甲状腺腫は、みるみる消えていっています。神をほめたたえます！

MR. PAUL DE MEURERS, Phila., Pa.

## 目を見張る証し
A STRIKING TESTIMONY.

（ここには、ノラ・スチューバーの証しが載っているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）

After being a great sufferer for seven years, and having undergone eight operations, none of which were successful, I gave up in despair, because an open sore in my side for four years caused me untold agony. This, the doctors said, would eventually result in cancer or consumption, causing death between the age of thirty and forty years.

My sufferings and fears cannot be imagined. A specialist suggested another operation, and I was almost ready to yield to instruments again, when a dear Christian girl pointed me to the Great Physician. The Lord opened the way for me to attend meetings for divine healing, conducted by Mrs. Etter in Philadelphia, Pa., during August, 1911; and while there I accepted healing by faith.

Mrs. Etter laid hands on me, and in the name of Jesus the devil was cast from my body. All glory to Him, for victory is mine through the precious Blood of Jesus. Hallelujah!

My spiritual blessings can scarcely be described, for the power of God came upon me so mightily that I became unconscious for hours, and during that time I was carried to the Great White Throne. Oh, the joy of my soul! Hallelujah! There I wanted to stay, but in my vision Jesus lovingly commanded me to return and tell the suffering world what had been done for me; so that others might know and believe. Glory to Jesus! He is just the same today as when He walked in Galilee --ever willing, ever able, to help those who put their trust in Him. Glory be to God for what He has done for me.

NORA STUBER, 643 Locust St., Reading, Pa.

## 夫の死
MY HUSBAND PASSES AWAY.

In the midst of this meeting, on a Sunday afternoon, while I was preaching, my husband, S. P. Etter, fell asleep in Jesus. I took a fast train to Indianapolis, laid him in the beautiful Crown Hill Cemetery, and got back to Philadelphia to continue the meeting the following Saturday night.
この集会のさなか、ある日曜日の午後、私が説教をしていたとき、夫S・P・エターがイエスにあって眠りについた。私は急行列車でインディアナポリスへ行き、美しいクラウンヒル墓地に夫を葬った。そして次の土曜日の夜には、集会を続けるためにフィラデルフィアに戻った。

He passed away triumphant. At his request they sang, "Jesus, Savior, Pilot Me." Waving his hands towards heaven and the saints, he passed over the tide. (See page 396.)
夫は勝利のうちに世を去った。夫の願いで、人々は「救い主イエスよ、私の水先案内となってください」を歌った。夫は天と聖徒たちに向かって手を振りながら、潮を越えて行った（第五十章（その二）を参照）。
