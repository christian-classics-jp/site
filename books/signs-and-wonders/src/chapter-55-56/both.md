# 第五十五章　カンザス州トピーカに火が下る
CHAPTER LV
THE FIRE FALLS AT TOPEKA, KANSAS

## 敵が騒ぎ立つ　人々は祝福される
ENEMY GETS STIRRED. PEOPLE BLESSED. A GREAT

## 大いなる勝利が得られる
Victory Won

For two years Bro. Foster and the saints had been calling me to Topeka to hold a convention, and at last we felt that the Lord was leading, so the last of July, 1915, I, with several good workers, landed in the beautiful city of Topeka. Many of my readers have read of the great meeting we had in that city twenty-seven years ago, when I went with two young girls and a janitor as strangers, at the invitation of one minister, and held meetings for nearly two months in the beautiful park. My large tent, that held nearly eight thousand, had been given to me in Oakland, California, was destroyed by a storm. Thousands of people came out and saw we were trying to hold on in the grove. They went to work at once, raised money and bought me a large new tent. Now, after all these many years, I find myself again holding meetings in the large city park theater, with merry-go-rounds, dance halls, all kinds of sports and amusements around. The dear Lord Jesus says, "If I be lifted up, I shall draw all men unto Me." He is the great drawing power. As we preached the Word giving Him the pre-eminence above all men and powers, the Lord was with us, confirming the word with "signs and wonders." They began to bring the sick on beds and crutches, lame, blind and the halt were seen running from their cots and beds, and the blind were made to see, the lame leaping and shouting. Sinners wept their way to Calvary and arising and praising the Lord, for they had found Him precious to their souls. Many times the places of amusement were empty and the great theater was crowded and many could not get in. Many ministers of different faiths came from all parts on trains, in companies, bringing the sick with them
二年のあいだ、フォスター兄弟と聖徒たちは、大会を開くために私をトピーカに招き続けていた。ついに私たちは主が導いておられると感じた。そこで一九一五年七月の末、私は何人かのよい働き人と共に、美しい町トピーカに着いた。読者の多くは、二十七年前にその町で開いた大集会のことを読んだだろう。そのとき私は、ひとりの牧師に招かれ、ふたりの若い娘と雑用係を連れて、見知らぬ者として出かけて行った。そして美しい公園で、二か月近く集会を開いた。カリフォルニア州オークランドで贈られた、八千人近くが入る私の大天幕は、嵐で壊されてしまった。何千人もの人々がやって来て、私たちが林の中でなんとか集会を続けようとしているのを見た。彼らはすぐに動き出し、お金を集めて、私に大きな新しい天幕を買ってくれた。それから長い年月を経た今、私は再び市の公園の大きな劇場で集会を開いている。周りには回転木馬やダンスホール、あらゆる種類の運動や娯楽がある。愛する主イエスは、『わたしがこの地から上げられる時には、すべての人をわたしのところに引きよせるであろう』と言われる（ヨハネ一二・三二）。主こそ、人を引き寄せる大いなる力である。私たちが、すべての人とすべての力にまさる第一の地位を主にささげてみことばを宣べ伝えると、主は私たちと共にいて、「しるしと不思議」をもってみことばを確かなものとしてくださった。人々は、寝床に寝かせたり松葉杖をつかせたりして、病人を連れて来はじめた。足の不自由な人、目の見えない人、足なえが、簡易寝台や寝床から走り出すのが見られた。目の見えない人は見えるようになり、足の不自由な人は跳びはね、叫んだ。罪人たちは涙を流しながらカルバリに行き、立ち上がって主をほめたたえた。主が自分の魂にとって尊いお方であることを見いだしたからである。娯楽の場所が空になり、大きな劇場が満員になって多くの人が入れないことが、何度もあった。さまざまな教派の多くの牧師たちが、病人を連れて、列車で群れをなして各地からやって来た。

All this was voiced abroad and there was a great stir. The brethren had been having meetings in the city for years; they were not liked and some had a prejudice against them. When they saw the great success, interest and large crowds, they said, "we will stop this," so they began to look for something to accuse them of. They found a child at the altar lying under the power of God, and they passed a decree that if they found another child under sixteen at the altar they would arrest the parents and close the meeting. A woman brought her son over a hundred miles to be healed. The doctors could not do anything for him, and the mother was also very badly diseased. They were both healed the first day.
このことはすべて広く言い伝えられ、大騒ぎになった。兄弟たちは何年も前からこの町で集会を開いていたが、好かれておらず、彼らに偏見を持つ者もいた。大きな成功と関心の高まりと大群衆を見て、その者たちは「これをやめさせてやる」と言い、兄弟たちを訴える口実を探しはじめた。彼らは、ひとりの子供が神の力の下で祭壇に横たわっているのを見つけた。そして、十六歳未満の子供がまた祭壇にいるのを見つけたら、親を逮捕し集会を閉鎖する、という命令を出した。ある女性が、いやしを受けさせるために、百マイル以上の道のりを息子を連れてやって来た。医者たちは息子に何もしてやれず、母親もひどい病気にかかっていた。ふたりとも最初の日にいやされた。

## 警官が祭壇から少年をつかみ去る
OFFICER GRABS BOY FROM THE ALTAR

That night the boy went with his mother to the altar to seek the baptism in the Holy Ghost. The police went from one end of the long altar to the other, which was filled with men and women. Down at the end they found this boy kneeling with his mother. They grabbed him up away from his mother, who tried to hold him, and took him to the police station. The next day they told the mother to send the boy home and if he went back to the meeting, they would arrest them both and have to pay a fine. They were from another county and could have sued the city for heavy damages. After all this fright the boy was perfectly healed. They told the leaders of the meeting that they must leave the grounds at once. We had many small tents, and we could not move in a minute. That night the crowds had gathered and they had said that they would turn out the lights at 8 o'clock. Well, the lights went out and we were left in darkness. A man at this time stepped forward and said that he had a lovely grove, just across the way, and we could have it free of charge, and he would see what the city would do with him, and we could stay as long as we wanted to.
その夜、少年は聖霊のバプテスマを求めて、母親と共に祭壇に出た。長い祭壇は男女でいっぱいだったが、警官たちはその端から端まで歩いて回った。その端で、彼らはこの少年が母親と共にひざまずいているのを見つけた。母親は息子を抱きとめようとしたが、彼らは少年を母親から引き離して、警察署へ連れて行った。翌日、彼らは母親に、少年を家に帰らせるよう命じた。もし少年が集会に戻れば、ふたりとも逮捕して罰金を払わせる、というのである。母子はほかの郡から来ていたので、市を相手に多額の損害賠償を求めて訴えることもできた。こんなに怖い思いをしたあとでも、少年は完全にいやされていた。彼らは集会の指導者たちに、すぐにその場所から出て行くよう命じた。私たちには小さな天幕がたくさんあったので、すぐに移ることはできなかった。その夜は群衆が集まっていたが、彼らは八時に明かりを消すと言っていた。はたして明かりが消え、私たちは暗やみの中に取り残された。このとき、ひとりの男が進み出て言った。道のすぐ向かいに自分のすてきな林があるから、無料で使ってよい。市が自分をどうするか見てやろう。好きなだけそこにいてよい、と。

## 牧師が逮捕され、釈放される
PASTOR ARRESTED AND RELEASED

The officers were raging and arrested the leader, took him to the station, but he was soon bailed out. I expected them to take me next. I went through a martydom the next two or three days, looking continually to be arrested. I carried every day a kimono and some little things I would need in jail. But they were not after me; they never spoke against me at all. If you follow the steps of Jesus closely you will know how the martyrs of old suffered and were persecuted. The whole city was stirred, and everywhere they were either for or against the meeting. Oh! Glory to God! Christ was Victor. He was leading the hosts of the Lord. A large tent was secured at once, the small ones were soon removed to a much better place than we had before. The crowds increased continually, also the interest. Many of the best citizens and church members that had taken no part or interest, were very indignant at the way we had been treated and came out to see. The tent was crowded, people were standing packed all around the tent and so still that all could hear. The power of God came like a cloud and rested over the tent and grounds, and the fear and presence of God was felt by all. Everyone seemed to respect us, and ministers continued to come to the help of the Lord. Many who had never seen the mighty "signs and wonders" God is giving these last days, saw the sick healed of the worst diseases, the deaf made to hear, and the lame to walk, and the blind to see. They said it looked like the days of Christ in Galilee, and when they saw all classes--old men and women and little children--speaking in "other languages," and giving the true interpretations, all telling the wonderful works of God, they were confounded and said, "What meaneth this? Surely we have seen strange things!"
警官たちは怒り狂い、指導者を逮捕して署に連れて行ったが、彼は間もなく保釈された。次は私が連れて行かれるだろうと思った。それから二、三日、私はいつ逮捕されるかと絶えず思いながら、殉教のような苦しみを味わった。私は毎日、部屋着と、牢屋で必要になりそうなこまごまとした物を持ち歩いた。しかし、彼らがねらっていたのは私ではなかった。私に反対することは一言も言わなかった。イエスの足跡にぴったりと従うなら、昔の殉教者たちがどのように苦しみ、迫害されたかがわかるだろう。町全体が騒然となり、至る所で人々は集会に賛成するか反対するかに分かれた。ああ、神に栄光があるように。キリストは勝利者であられた。キリストが主の軍勢を導いておられたのである。すぐに大きな天幕が確保され、小さな天幕も間もなく、前よりずっとよい場所に移された。群衆は増え続け、関心も高まり続けた。それまで加わることも関心を持つこともなかった、りっぱな市民や教会員の多くが、私たちの受けた扱いにひどく憤り、見に来た。天幕は満員で、人々は天幕の周りにぎっしりと立っていた。みなが聞き取れるほど、あたりは静まり返っていた。神の力が雲のように来て、天幕と敷地の上にとどまった。すべての人が、神への畏れと神の臨在を感じた。だれもが私たちに敬意を払っているようだった。牧師たちも、主を助けに次々とやって来た。神がこの終わりの時に与えておられる力ある「しるしと不思議」を一度も見たことのなかった多くの人々が、それを見た。病人が最悪の病からいやされ、耳の聞こえない人が聞こえるようになり、足の不自由な人が歩き、目の見えない人が見えるようになったのである。彼らは、ガリラヤでのキリストの日々のようだと言った。また、年老いた男女から小さな子供まで、あらゆる人々が「他国の言葉」で語り、正しい解き明かしをし、みな神の大きな働きを述べるのを見た。彼らは驚きあきれて、「これは、いったい、どういうわけなのだろう。確かに、きょうは驚くべきことを見た」と言った。
// 英文の「martydom」は「martyrdom」の誤植。群衆の言葉は、使徒二・一二 とルカ五・二六 の口語訳の文言を借りた。

Sister Oaks, a poor woman, with three little children to care for, on the camp grounds gave many strong messages in "other tongues," with a voice like thunder. She was very small and frail, and she would pray and sing in the Spirit in "other tongues." She, with others, would join in the holy dance. They were so light that it seemed that they would float away. The holy fire spread far and near and eternity alone will tell the mighty work God wrought in Topeka.
オークス姉妹は、三人の小さな子供を世話しなければならない貧しい女性だったが、野営地で雷のような声をもって、「異言」で多くの力強い言葉を語った。彼女はたいへん小柄できゃしゃだったが、御霊によって「異言」で祈り、歌った。彼女はほかの人々と共に、聖なる踊りに加わった。彼らはあまりに軽やかで、今にも浮かんで行ってしまいそうだった。聖なる火は遠くにも近くにも広がった。神がトピーカで成し遂げられた力あるわざは、永遠だけが明らかにするだろう。

Notes on the camp-meeting held in Topeka, Kans., during the month of August, 1915, by Sister Etter and workers, Brother and Sister Howell, Bro. Feick and Sister Ida Ball.

There were about three hundred people from many different States and from Canada in attendance.

Many testified to healing whose names we never learned. The power of God was wonderful. As songs of praise went up to God, the power fell and singing and dancing in the Spirit was a feature in almost every meeting.

Sister Etter is an illustration of the power of God upon one's life. Although she has reached the age of three score and ten, she is remarkably preserved. It seemed the Holy Spirit prompted all she said. "Truly grace fell from her lips." Her prayer for God to "drop down something for the people to take away" was answered in every meeting.

Had not the enemy divided the saints in Topeka over a false doctrine, God could have worked even more blessedly than He did.

Many blessed messages in tongues and interpretation were given.

Eternity alone will reveal the results of this meeting.

HEALINGS

Mrs. Ella Tunnelly, a colored woman, North Tokepa, Kans., was healed of a tumor of four or five years' standing, and heart trouble. She had to walk with a crutch. When healed, she was able to jump. Her mother and sister were watching to catch her, for they couldn't understand the instantaneous healing she had received. She said, "I ain't tired. If I keep on feeling this way, I'll never die. I'm so glad I've come I don't know what to do."

This precious sister was so filled with the Holy Spirit and the joy of God, that she talked for about three-quarters of an hour, and the people listened, spellbound, for she spoke with the unction of the Holy Ghost.

Mrs. Geo. H. Hough, 1707 Prospect Ave., Kansas City, Mo., for four years troubled in her side from a dislocated rib, caused from lifting a big piece of coal, was healed by the power of God.

Mrs. P. M. La Burge, Roosevelt, Okla., testified to healing of consumption with which she had been troubled more or less for twenty-two years. While Sister Etter was praying for her, she told her to praise the Lord instead of coughing.

How blessedly He reveals Himself to us by His Spirit! Himself took our infirmities and bare our diseases.

Mrs. Stewart, of Beatrice, Neb., was healed of tuberculosis of glands of the neck. She couldn't raise her left arm. Her skin was almost yellow. She was healed instantly. All praise to Jesus.

Rebecca Templeton, a colored woman, of North Topeka, crippled for nine years, had tried doctors and medicines and linaments of every kind and couldn't be cured. In fact, tried every remedy anybody told her to. The Lord healed her.

Mrs. N. E. Porter, 1115 North Madison Street, received healing and the baptism of the Holy Spirit. The glory of God filled her soul and she shouted and praised the Lord and talked in tongues. God also gave her the gift of interpretation.

Mrs. Brayfield had used no medicine for 8 years. She was healed of rheumatism. She had lost the sight of one eye, but God is restoring sight. Mr. Schultz, Dearfield, O., read Sister Etter's book and went to Chicago and was healed when given up by doctors. He and his wife came a thousand miles to attend the camp-meeting. Mrs. Schultz received a healing from stiffness in her knees, from which she had suffered for years, while she was lying under the mighty power of God. She lay there for hours.

Mrs. Strommer, Montevidea, Minn., was brought in a dying condition with cancer of the stomach. She was reduced to a mere skeleton, She could not have lived but a few days longer, apparently. After being prayed for, she walked back and forth in front of the rostrum praising God. She said she had a new life from God as soon as she was prayed for.

Rev. J. L. Bishop, Pomona, Kans., was healed of hay fever and received his baptism with the Holy Ghost. He was a Congregationalist preacher.

Earl Rush, Cedar, Kans., was healed of weak ankles. He walked four miles the next day after his healing. He was saved two years ago when a drunkard and addicted to the use of tobacco. He was healed of appendicitis then, too. His ankles had been weak from childhood.

Miss Louise Albach, Scranton, Kans., praised God for what her eyes saw of the power of God in these meetings; for the liberty of the Spirit which she enjoyed, and for the divine touch she received when Sister Etter prayed for her. Miss Ada Gilbert, near Country Club, Topeka, Kans., testified to God's healing power in restoring her sight. She had weak eyes as a child and had to give up school work at the age of twelve. The power of God rested upon this dear sister during meetings and her testimonies showed a deeper sinking into Jesus day after day. God gave her the assurance that her sight would be entirely restored.

## カンザス州トピーカで多くの人がいやされる
MANY HEALED AT TOPEKA, KAN.

（ここには、アンドルー・L・ターナーの手紙が載っているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）

Kiefer, Okla., August 12, 1915.

Dear Editor: Greeting in Jesus' precious name. I just returned from the Topeka, Kan., Camp-meeting, conducted by our beloved Sister M. B. Woodworth-Etter. Glory to God. The Lord is working in mighty power. Sinners weeping accept salvation, believers receiving the Holy Ghost as in Acts 2:4 and 19:6; the sick and afflicted shouting complete healing. Praise His Holy Name. Sick and afflicted from many States are there at the end of the first week and almost every train brings others, coming for healing. I will give a short sketch below of what I witnessed: Mrs. R. S. Paxton, Topeka, nervous prostration nine months, given up by five doctors, one specialist said the asylum was the only resort; instantly healed.

This is only one of the many marvelous works of God I beheld the short time I was there. Glory to God!

ANDREW L. TURNER.

# 第五十六章　コロラド州コロラドスプリングズ
CHAPTER LVI
COLORADO SPRINGS, COLO.

## コロラド州プエブロでの数日も
ALSO A FEW DAYS AT PUEBLO, COLO.

The work that God did at our meeting at Topeka, Kans., began to spread far and near. Los Angeles, Cal., where we held the World-wide Camp-Meeting just a few years before, was the next field of labor that we had promised.
カンザス州トピーカでの私たちの集会で神がなさったわざは、遠くにも近くにも広がりはじめた。次に約束していた働きの場は、ほんの数年前に世界野営集会を開いたカリフォルニア州ロサンゼルスだった。

In the meantime the saints at Colorado Springs, Colo., learned that we were to pass through their city and that we had a little time left before our meeting in Los Angeles began. They sent in urgent requests to us; "Real Macedonian Cries" for us not to pass them by without giving them a meeting, and deal out to them the Bread of Life. Although I needed rest so badly, I felt that I could not pass by this needy field of labor, and we promised them a few weeks' meeting. When the saints received my assurance that I was coming, they at once began to cry to God for good weather. They had rain almost every day for a long time, making it almost impossible to hold a meeting in a tent. God wonderfully heard and answered their prayers. The day the meeting started it stopped raining and did not rain again until the day after we closed, except for one little shower, which lasted only a few minutes while the meeting was on. This little shower showed us how helpless we would have been had not God checked the rain, as the tent let the water through so that we had to use umbrellas.
そのあいだに、コロラド州コロラドスプリングズの聖徒たちが、私たちが自分たちの町を通ることと、ロサンゼルスでの集会が始まるまでに少し時間があることを知った。彼らは私たちに切実な願いを送って来た。それは「まことのマケドニヤの叫び」だった。集会を開かずに素通りしないで、いのちのパンを分け与えてほしい、というのである。私はひどく休息を必要としていたが、この助けを必要としている働きの場を素通りすることはできないと感じた。そして、数週間の集会を約束した。私が行くという確約を受け取ると、聖徒たちはすぐに、よい天気を求めて神に叫びはじめた。長いあいだほとんど毎日雨が降っていて、天幕で集会を開くのはほとんど不可能だったのである。神は彼らの祈りをすばらしく聞き、答えてくださった。集会が始まった日に雨はやみ、集会を閉じた翌日まで再び降ることはなかった。例外は、集会中にほんの数分だけ降った一度の小雨だけだった。この小雨で、もし神が雨を止めてくださらなかったら、私たちがどんなに無力だったかがわかった。天幕から雨がもって、傘をささなければならなかったのである。

God greatly blessed us while the meeting lasted. They all told us that it was a hard field of labor, little having been accomplished for God all summer. We found out that this was so, but nothing is too hard for God, if we only trust Him and keep looking up to him.
集会が続いたあいだ、神は私たちを大いに祝福してくださった。そこは難しい働きの場で、夏のあいだ神のために成し遂げられたことはほとんどない、とだれもが私たちに言った。実際そのとおりだとわかった。しかし、私たちが神に信頼し、神を仰ぎ続けさえすれば、神にとって難しすぎることは何もない。

The meeting was not extensively advertised, as we came on short notice. Just a few days before we arrived the minister whose field of labor it was, and whom we expected to stand right by us, got an urgent call to help in another field. This left us practically alone, but God was still with us.
急に来ることになったので、集会は広く宣伝されていなかった。私たちが着くほんの数日前、そこを働きの場とし、私たちをそばで支えてくれると期待していた牧師に、ほかの場所を助けてほしいという急な招きが来た。そのため私たちはほとんど孤立したが、それでも神は私たちと共にいてくださった。

The crowds were not so very large to begin with, but some real hungry, needy souls were present, who soon got filled with God. Some of the mightiest miracles were performed daily by the sword of the Spirit.
群衆は、初めはそれほど多くなかった。しかし、本当に飢え渇き、助けを必要としている魂がいくらか来ていて、間もなく神に満たされた。御霊の剣によって、毎日、最も力ある奇跡のいくつかが行われた。

## 鉱夫の肺病がいやされる
MINER'S CONSUMPTION HEALED

A man was dying with Miner's consumption. The doctors gave him only a few months to live. His wife told him to go down to those meetings and see what could be done for him. He almost went in a rage at the thought of going to a religious meeting, especially down in that tent. But the disease was playing havoc with his body. He knew his time on earth was limited. He did decide to go down one day. He took a seat well up towards the front. Before the customary time for prayer for the sick arrived, the Spirit pointed this man out to me. I went down to him, a perfect stranger to me, and began to deal with and pray for him. It was not long before the power of God struck him like lightning. He threw off his coat and cried, "I am saved and healed." He began to demonstrate by pounding his lungs hard with his fist. He did this day after day, prayed, testified and made himself very useful in the meeting. The change in him was marvelous. His appetite returned. His wife could hardly cook enough for him.
ひとりの男が、鉱夫の肺病で死にかけていた。医者たちは、あと数か月のいのちだと言った。妻は彼に、あの集会に行って、どうにかしてもらえないか見ておいでと言った。宗教の集会に、それもあの天幕に行くなどと考えただけで、彼は怒り出しそうになった。しかし、病は彼の体をひどくむしばんでいた。自分の地上での時が限られていることを、彼は知っていた。それで、ある日、彼はとうとう行くことに決めた。彼はかなり前のほうの席に着いた。いつもの病人のための祈りの時間が来る前に、御霊がこの人を私に指し示された。私は、まったく見知らぬこの人のところへ下りて行き、彼に語りかけ、彼のために祈りはじめた。間もなく、神の力が稲妻のように彼を打った。彼は上着を脱ぎ捨てて、「私は救われ、いやされた」と叫んだ。そして、こぶしで胸を強くたたいて、それを示しはじめた。彼は来る日も来る日もそうして、祈り、証しをし、集会でたいへん役に立つ働きをした。彼の変わりようは驚くばかりだった。食欲が戻り、妻は彼が食べるだけの料理を作るのに追いつかないほどだった。

## 耳が聞こえず口のきけない霊が出て行く
DEAF AND DUMB SPIRIT GOES OUT

I noticed a sweet woman about forty years of age look so earnestly at me day after day, well back in the audience. I asked myself the question: Why does she not "praise the Lord," or testify. She does not seem to say a word at any time. Later on they brought her on the platform to be prayed for and told me she was deaf and dumb from birth. I understood it all then. We prayed for God to open the deaf ears and loosen the tongue, and in a short time she heard the music and began to rejoice with us. You never saw a sweeter and brighter face than hers when she heard and made sounds.
会衆のかなり後ろのほうから、来る日も来る日もじっと私を見つめている、四十歳ほどの優しげな女性がいることに、私は気づいた。私は自分に問いかけた。なぜあの人は「主をほめたたえ」もせず、証しもしないのだろうか。いつも一言も話さないようだ。あとで人々が、祈ってもらうために彼女を壇に連れて来て、彼女が生まれつき耳が聞こえず口がきけないのだと私に告げた。それで私はすべてがわかった。私たちは、神が聞こえない耳を開き、舌を解いてくださるよう祈った。すると間もなく、彼女は音楽を聞き、私たちと共に喜びはじめた。音を聞き、声を出したときの彼女の顔ほど、優しく輝いた顔は見たことがない。

## さまざまな病が去る
VARIOUS DISEASES GO

One woman was brought in suffering so severely with appendicitis that she was in a screaming condition. She had been in a bad state for about six weeks. Nothing but an operation would have saved her life, outside of the power of God. When I prayed for her the pain instantly left her, and she got saved. She got complete deliverance.
ひとりの女性が連れて来られた。虫垂炎がひどく、叫び声を上げるほど苦しんでいた。六週間ほどのあいだ、悪い状態が続いていた。神の力のほかには、手術しか彼女のいのちを救う道はなかっただろう。私が祈ると、痛みはたちまち去り、彼女は救われた。彼女は完全に解放された。

Many people were brought in on cots, suffering with all kinds of diseases. Sometimes the odor was offensive, but God touched and healed every one I believe. It was marvelous the way the sick and afflicted got up and walked in this meeting. God's power was present to heal.
多くの人が、あらゆる病に苦しみながら、簡易寝台で運び込まれた。ときには悪臭がしたが、神はひとり残らず触れて、いやしてくださったと私は信じている。この集会で、病人や苦しむ人々が起き上がって歩いた様子は、驚くべきものだった。いやす神の力がそこにあった。

Another woman who was suffering with a complication of diseases, mostly hardening of the arteries. She had been helpless for some time; she was lying on a cot waiting her turn when I laid hands on her and put the blood of Jesus over her. She immediately went to sleep and when she woke up she smiled and said that all pain was gone. She began to get happy and praise God for what He had done for her.
また、ひとりの女性は、いくつもの病の合併に苦しんでいた。その多くは動脈硬化によるものだった。彼女はしばらく前から寝たきりだった。簡易寝台に横たわって順番を待っていたとき、私は彼女に手を置き、イエスの血で彼女を覆った。彼女はすぐに眠りに落ち、目を覚ますと、ほほえんで、痛みはすべて消えたと言った。彼女は喜びにあふれ、神がしてくださったことのゆえに神をほめたたえはじめた。

The interest and crowds increased as the people began to find out what God was doing in the tent. The power of God was being demonstrated, the people getting saved, healed and baptized. Some of the saints came as far as forty miles, from Pueblo, Colo., in their autos, to be in the meeting. God greatly blessed them, some receiving their baptism, and began to glorify God in other tongues.
神が天幕で何をしておられるのかを人々が知りはじめると、関心も群衆も増していった。神の力が目に見える形で示され、人々は救われ、いやされ、バプテスマを受けた。聖徒たちの中には、集会に出るために、四十マイルも離れたコロラド州プエブロから自動車で来た者もいた。神は彼らを大いに祝福してくださった。バプテスマを受けた者もいて、異言で神をあがめはじめた。

Some prominent people attended this little meeting. Millionaires were among our company and sought God with us. All felt sorry that our time was so limited. Sinners told us "you should not leave yet, as people are just beginning to find out what is taking place." In a short time if you stay the tent won't hold the people. We felt this was true, but our next meeting made it necessary for us to move to our next field of labor, out West.
この小さな集会に、何人かの著名な人々も出席した。百万長者たちも私たちの中にいて、私たちと共に神を求めた。だれもが、私たちの時間がこれほど限られていることを残念がった。罪人たちは私たちに言った。「まだ去るべきではない。何が起こっているのか、人々がちょうど知りはじめたところだ。とどまれば、間もなく天幕に人が入りきらなくなる」そのとおりだと私たちも感じたが、次の集会のために、西部の次の働きの場へ移らなければならなかった。

Many books were scattered in this little place. On the day we left one of my workers entered a little shoe shop to get his shoes repaired. On entering the man said to him, "You are having wonderful meetings down there in the tent." The answer was, "Yes, sir, we are." The man then said, "I got one of your books the other night and here it is lying open right by me. I have read it half way through already. My, it is wonderful the way the power of God is demonstrated. I believe this is what we all ought to have. My wife is a Presbyterian. She is very quiet. I know this baptism of the Holy Ghost is what we both need." Soon others entered the shop who had practically the same thoughts in their hearts. "We need more of God, too. You people pray for us."
この小さな町に、多くの本が広まった。私たちが去る日、私の働き人のひとりが、靴を直してもらおうと小さな靴屋に入った。入るとすぐ、店の主人が彼に言った。「あの天幕では、すばらしい集会をやっていますね」答えは、「はい、そうです」だった。すると主人は言った。「先日の夜、あなたがたの本を一冊手に入れましてね。ほら、ここに開いて置いてあります。もう半分まで読みました。いやあ、神の力がこんなふうに現されるとは、すばらしいものです。これこそ、私たちみんなが持つべきものだと思います。家内は長老派で、とてももの静かです。この聖霊のバプテスマこそ、私たちふたりに必要なものだと思っています」間もなく、ほとんど同じ思いを心に抱いた人々が、ほかにも店に入って来た。「私たちにも、もっと神が必要です。あなたがた、私たちのために祈ってください」

## プエブロの聖徒たちが祝福される
PUEBLO SAINTS GET BLESSED

The saints that came up from Pueblo, Colo., gave us no rest till we promised them a few days' meeting at least. They said we will bring you to Pueblo in an auto, so it won't cost you a cent. Pueblo is on your way to Los Angeles. It seemed almost impossible to refuse. We promised them and had a nice trip to their city, seeing the beautiful country, although it was very hard on me.
コロラド州プエブロから来た聖徒たちは、せめて数日の集会を約束するまで、私たちを放してくれなかった。自動車でプエブロまで連れて行くから一セントもかからない、プエブロはロサンゼルスへの途中にある、と彼らは言った。断ることはほとんどできないように思われた。私たちは約束し、美しい田園を眺めながら、彼らの町まで快い旅をした。もっとも、私の体にはたいへんこたえた。

God greatly blessed our little stay here with the precious saints. The power fell in a mighty way. I felt that if we could have stayed in this place for some time, a revival would have been the result. Some wonderful cases of healings took place. May God greatly bless their pastor, and the dear family who so kindly took us into their home and provided for us.
尊い聖徒たちとのここでの短い滞在を、神は大いに祝福してくださった。力が力強く下った。もしこの地にしばらくとどまれたなら、リバイバルが起こっただろうと私は感じた。すばらしいいやしもいくつか起こった。神が彼らの牧師と、親切にも私たちを家に迎えて世話をしてくれた愛する家族とを、大いに祝福してくださるように。

Both the saints at Colorado Springs and Pueblo, Colo., are calling us back to their field of labor for a regular campaign. I hope and pray that God will speedily open the way so that they will get their heart's desire.
コロラドスプリングズとプエブロの聖徒たちは、どちらも、本格的な伝道集会のために私たちを再び招いている。神がすみやかに道を開き、彼らが心の願いをかなえられるよう、私は望み、祈っている。

Brother A. E. Raiford of Eufaula, Okla., writes under date of April 1, 1916: "I saw people come there to be healed that were just about dead, and were healed and went away rejoicing. My remembrance of you will never be forgotten." This dear brother is out and out for God. The sights that he saw, how the sick were carried in and walked out, left a deep impression on him.
オクラホマ州ユーフォーラのA・E・レイフォード兄弟は、一九一六年四月一日付けでこう書いて来た。「死にかけていた人々がいやしを受けにそこへ来て、いやされ、喜びながら帰って行くのを見ました。あなたのことは決して忘れません」この愛する兄弟は、全身全霊で神のために生きている。病人が運び込まれては歩いて出て行く光景を見て、彼は深い感銘を受けたのである。
