# 第五十九章　一九一六年、サンフランシスコでの戦い
CHAPTER LIX
THE BATTLE AT SAN FRANCISCO, 1916

## リバイバルが広がる。教会から大きな劇場へ
REVIVAL SPREADS. WE MOVE FROM CHURCH TO BIG THEATRE
// 原本の小見出しが、OCR で段落の頭と同じ行に読まれている。
From San Diego we went to San Francisco. Brother Craig met us, and took us to his home of faith. There we were made to feel at home, and everything was done for our comfort. Many saints came to the home and we had blessed fellowship; the sweet peace of God was always present all over the house.
サンディエゴから、私たちはサンフランシスコへ行った。クレイグ兄弟が私たちを迎え、自分の信仰の家へ連れて行ってくれた。そこで私たちは、わが家にいるようにくつろがせてもらった。私たちが快く過ごせるよう、あらゆることがなされた。多くの聖徒たちがその家に来て、私たちは祝福された交わりを持った。神の甘い平安が、いつも家じゅうに満ちていた。

The meeting commenced with power. The saints had been praying much, and they made every sacrifice to push the battle through to victory. The Lord was present to confirm His Word with visible signs of His invisible Presence. The people came and heard, saw, and wondered. Fear and conviction fell day after day and the people were made to say, "Surely God is in our midst." The altar was crowded daily; they came from all parts, and were saved, healed, filled with God, and baptized in the Holy Ghost. When their friends saw what a change had taken place, many of them started at once to attend the meeting. The interest grew. People of all classes were saved, including Jews. One afternoon, as we entered the theatre where our meeting was held, the minister met us at the door. He told us that they had brought a woman in an auto, and that the last six dollars they had had been given him to bring her, and he would not wait for the meeting, and placed the sick woman in the aisle nearest the entrance. It was a sad sight. The woman had two little children and she had not walked alone for four years. She was afflicted with many diseases. Her husband was also badly diseased, and discouraged. She knew it was her last chance, so she had faith as we pointed her to the loving Saviour, and prayed the prayer of faith, and laid on hands, the unclean spirits left her, the healing virtue came and swept through and over her, and she knew she was healed. She stood up, walked and shouted. "Glory to God!" Her husband began to praise the Lord, and he, too, was healed. She took up her child and walked out to the auto. As I went forward to the platform, I saw them carry a woman that was helpless; she was from a hospital for incurables, had not stood or walked for seven years. I looked down at her and several other cripples.
集会は力をもって始まった。聖徒たちは多く祈っていた。そして、戦いを勝利まで押し進めるために、あらゆる犠牲を払った。主はそこにおられ、目に見えないご臨在の目に見えるしるしによって、みことばを確かなものとしてくださった。人々はやって来て、聞き、見て、驚いた。日ごとに畏れと罪の自覚が臨み、人々は「まことに、神が私たちのうちにおられる」と言わずにはいられなかった。祭壇は毎日人でいっぱいだった。人々は至る所から来て、救われ、いやされ、神に満たされ、聖霊のバプテスマを受けた。その友人たちは、どれほどの変化が起こったかを見ると、多くがすぐに集会に通いはじめた。関心は高まっていった。ユダヤ人を含め、あらゆる階層の人々が救われた。
// 「Surely God is in our midst」は第一コリント一四・二五の借用。
ある日の午後、集会を開いていた劇場に入ると、牧師が戸口で私たちを迎えた。牧師の話では、人々がひとりの女性を自動車で連れて来たという。彼らは手もとに残った最後の六ドルを、彼女を運ぶために運転手に払った。運転手は集会まで待ってはくれなかった。それで、病気の女性を入口にいちばん近い通路に置いたのだという。痛ましい光景だった。その女性には小さな子供がふたりいた。そして四年間、ひとりで歩いたことがなかった。多くの病に苦しんでいた。夫もひどい病気を患っており、気落ちしていた。
// 「given him」「he would not wait」の him、he は、女性を自動車で運んだ人と読んだ。
彼女は、これが最後の機会だと知っていた。それで、私たちが愛に満ちた救い主を指し示すと、信仰を持った。私たちが信仰による祈りをささげて手を置くと、汚れた霊どもは彼女から出て行った。いやしの力が臨み、彼女の全身を貫き、包んだ。彼女は自分がいやされたことを知った。彼女は立ち上がり、歩いて、叫んだ。「神に栄光あれ！」夫も主をほめたたえはじめ、彼もまたいやされた。彼女は子供を抱き上げ、歩いて自動車まで出て行った。
私が壇へ進んで行くと、人々が体の自由のきかないひとりの女性を運んで来るのが見えた。彼女は不治の病人のための病院から来ていた。七年間、立つことも歩くこともできなかった。私は壇の上から、彼女とほかの何人かの足の不自由な人たちを見下ろした。

The Lord told me to rebuke her sickness and command her to get up and walk, that she was healed The power of the Holy Ghost came on me and I commanded her to arise, and walk in the name of Jesus. She looked at me, then began to try to move and she stood up then. I told her to walk out, and come up those steps. Then I came down about three steps from the platform, and caught her hand, then she walked up to and over the platform, like a girl. Those that brought her were frightened at first, thinking she was going to die, but when they realized she was really healed, they wept for joy, and told how helpless she had been; she could not turn in bed, for seven years.
主は私に、彼女の病をしかりつけ、起きて歩くように命じなさい、彼女はいやされたのだ、と言われた。聖霊の力が私に臨み、私はイエスの名によって、起きて歩くよう彼女に命じた。彼女は私を見た。それから体を動かそうとしはじめ、やがて立ち上がった。私は彼女に、歩いて出て来て、あの階段を上って来るように言った。それから私は壇から三段ほど降りて、彼女の手を取った。すると彼女は、少女のように壇まで上って来て、壇の上を歩いた。彼女を連れて来た人々は、初めは彼女が死ぬのではないかと思っておびえていた。しかし、本当にいやされたのだとわかると、喜びのあまり泣いた。そして、彼女がどれほど体の自由がきかなかったかを話した。七年間、寝床で寝返りを打つこともできなかったのである。
// 英文の「that she was healed The power」は、healed の後の句点が落ちていると読んだ。

Another lady who had been lame for ten years, began to cry and said, "Well, if she can walk I can, too." She arose and began to walk up and down; in less than twenty minutes, seven helpless cripples were walking through the aisles, and walked up the steps to the platform, I cannot find words to describe these miracles. I had never spoken to or laid hands on them, the Lord was proving that the power came from Him, and that He alone was doing the work of saving and healing. The congregation was moved, there was weeping and shouting all over the house. A man arose in the congregation and wanted to give testimony. Three years before when I was holding a meeting at San Jose, he came but did not know what was going on. He had to go inside. He had been a gambler and drunkard, was bad in every way, and was on the point of delirium tremens; the doctor told him he would die the next spell he had. He was miserable, and was almost ready to commit suicide. He had met with a bad accident; his nose was broken and his eye was puffed almost out on his cheek, his head and face was bandaged; it appeared like a hopeless case. He did not know why he went to the town, he was walking out but was not going to the meeting. Something drew him in. He arose and asked if there was any mercy, or help for him, and he yielded to God. In a short time he was saved, all pain left him, his sight was perfectly restored, his nose was healed and one little scar was left; he was delivered from all bad habits and was saved, kept by the power of God, and was very successful in the service of the Lord. His testimony was believed and had quite an effect on many; he was a great help. Praise the Lord.
十年間足が不自由だった別の婦人が、泣きだして言った。「まあ、あの人が歩けるのなら、私だって歩けます」彼女は立ち上がり、行ったり来たり歩きはじめた。二十分もたたないうちに、体の自由のきかない七人の足の不自由な人たちが通路を歩き、階段を上って壇まで来た。これらの奇跡を言い表す言葉が見つからない。私は彼らに話しかけたことも、手を置いたこともなかった。主は、力がご自分から出ていること、救いといやしのわざをなさっているのはご自分だけであることを、証明しておられたのである。会衆は心を動かされ、会場の至る所で泣く声と叫ぶ声が上がった。
会衆の中からひとりの男が立ち上がり、証しをしたいと言った。三年前、私がサンノゼで集会を開いていたとき、彼はやって来たが、何が行われているのかを知らなかった。それでも、中に入らずにはいられなかった。彼はばくち打ちで大酒飲みで、あらゆる点で悪い人間だった。酒のために錯乱を起こす寸前で、医者からは、次に発作が起きたら死ぬだろうと言われていた。彼はみじめで、もう少しで自らいのちを絶つところだった。ひどい事故にも遭っていた。鼻は折れ、片目は腫れて頬の上に飛び出しそうになっていた。頭と顔には包帯が巻かれていた。望みのない様子に見えた。
なぜ町へ出かけたのか、彼にはわからなかった。外を歩いていたが、集会に行くつもりはなかった。何かが彼を中へ引き入れたのである。彼は立ち上がって、自分にもあわれみや助けがあるのかと尋ね、神に身をゆだねた。間もなく彼は救われ、痛みはすべて去った。視力は完全に戻り、鼻もいやされて、小さな傷跡が一つ残っただけだった。彼はあらゆる悪い習慣から解き放たれ、救われて、神の力によって守られた。そして主の働きで大きな実を結んだ。彼の証しは信じられ、多くの人に少なからぬ影響を与えた。彼は大きな助けとなった。主をほめたたえよ。

The heavenly choir was great. Many had visions. Wonderful messages in tongues and interpretations were given. People came from nearly all the churches. Many got convicted for more of God, and that the coming of the Lord is very near. Many sought the baptism of the Holy Ghost. Dear brother and sister Montgomery were a great help to the work. Sister Piper, Dr. Smith and his wife, who stood so nobly by us all through the revival, twenty-seven years ago, joined us, and rendered valuable assistance during the whole campaign. May the dear Lord keep them many years to hold up the banner of Jesus. Eternity alone will tell what the Lord did in the meeting at San Francisco, California.
天の合唱はすばらしかった。多くの人が幻を見た。異言による驚くべき言葉と解き明かしが与えられた。ほとんどすべての教会から人々が来た。多くの人が、もっと神を求めなければならないこと、主の来臨がすぐ近くに迫っていることを、強く思い知らされた。多くの人が聖霊のバプテスマを求めた。愛するモンゴメリー兄弟姉妹は、働きの大きな助けとなった。パイパー姉妹と、スミス医師夫妻も加わり、伝道の全期間を通して貴重な助けを与えてくれた。スミス医師夫妻は、二十七年前のリバイバルのあいだずっと、私たちをりっぱに支えてくれた人たちである。愛する主が、彼らを長く守り、イエスの旗を高く掲げさせてくださるように。カリフォルニア州サンフランシスコの集会で主が何をなさったかは、永遠だけが明らかにするだろう。

## カリフォルニア州サンフランシスコでのウッドワース・エター夫人の集会（一月十三日から二月十三日、あるいはそれ以上）
MRS. WOODWORTH-ETTER'S MEETING AT SAN FRANCISCO, CAL.,
JANUARY 13TH TO FEBRUARY 13TH OR LONGER

(From The Pentecostal Herald.)
（『ペンテコスタル・ヘラルド』誌から転載）

Heaven is coming down to San Francisco, a notably wicked city. It is our honest conviction that God has chosen this Sodom of America to show forth some of the most startling miracles of the age to convince, if possible, the people that Jesus still lives and to give all a chance to get ready for the coming of the Bridegroom. At this date, January 20th, the convention is in full swing, with two daily meetings in the Zion M. E. church. The pastor has given over the beautiful new and ideally appointed church building for an old-fashioned Pentecostal revival. The revival opened up strong from the beginning, and as if not a moment could be lost, God began at the very first meeting to pour out His Spirit, bringing salvation and healing. Every meeting thus far has been a notable one for this city. The crowds are steadily increasing. It is now plainly evident that the commodious church will be too small for the people desiring to hear the glorious Gospel message and to witness the signs immediately following, as promised. Practically all who enter are stricken with awe at the conscious presence of God. People get healed in their seats. Others get healed and saved at the same time, when the prayer of faith is offered. One lady while in prayer for her daughter, who was being prayed for, was healed herself. The long altar is too small for those seeking God for salvation, healing and the baptism. San Francisco is a cosmopolitan city. Almost every nation under the sun has a strong representation here. Many nationalities are pressing in for God's best. Many Spanish-speaking people are being healed, saved, and some filled with the Spirit. All glory to our God and His Christ.
天がサンフランシスコに下って来ようとしている。ここは悪名高い邪悪な町である。私たちは心から確信している。神はアメリカのこのソドムをお選びになり、この時代の最も驚くべき奇跡のいくつかを現そうとしておられる。それは、できることなら人々に、イエスが今も生きておられることを納得させ、すべての人に花婿の来臨に備える機会を与えるためである。この日付、一月二十日の時点で、大会はたけなわである。シオン・メソジスト監督教会で、毎日二回の集会が開かれている。牧師は、美しく新しい、申し分のない設備の会堂を、昔ながらのペンテコステのリバイバルのために明け渡してくれた。
// M. E.＝Methodist Episcopal（メソジスト監督教会）。
リバイバルは初めから力強く始まった。神は一刻も無駄にできないかのように、最初の集会から御霊を注ぎはじめられ、救いといやしをもたらされた。これまでのどの集会も、この町にとって注目すべきものだった。群衆は着実に増えている。栄光の福音のメッセージを聞き、約束どおりすぐに伴うしるしを見たいと願う人々には、この広い会堂も狭すぎるようになる。そのことが、今やはっきりしている。入って来る人のほとんどすべてが、神のご臨在をありありと感じて畏れに打たれる。座席に座ったままいやされる人々もいる。信仰による祈りがささげられると、いやされると同時に救われる人々もいる。ひとりの婦人は、娘のために祈りがささげられているあいだ自分も祈っていて、自分自身がいやされた。長い祭壇も、救いといやしとバプテスマを求めて神を求める人々には狭すぎる。
サンフランシスコは国際的な都市である。天下のほとんどすべての国の人々が、ここに大勢いる。多くの国の人々が、神の最善のものを求めて押し寄せている。スペイン語を話す多くの人々がいやされ、救われている。御霊に満たされた人々もいる。すべての栄光を、私たちの神とそのキリストに。

No disease is hard for our Christ when the saints are dwelling in sweet unity, as is seen here at present. The word goes forth with no uncertain sound. We are feeling the necessity in Glad Tidings Assembly of having the "signs following" to show forth the real full Gospel. Let every reader offer believing prayer that "all may be one, even as Christ and the Father are one." (John 17-21.) For them His glory shall shine forth.
聖徒たちが、今ここに見られるように甘い一致のうちに住んでいるとき、私たちのキリストにとって難しい病は一つもない。みことばは、はっきりとした音をもって出て行く。グラッド・タイディングズ・アセンブリーで、私たちは、真の完全な福音を示すために「伴うしるし」が必要であることを感じている。読者のひとりひとりが、「キリストと父とが一つであられるように、みなが一つとなるように」と、信じて祈りをささげていただきたい（ヨハネ一七・二一）。そのような人々のために、主の栄光は輝き出るであろう。

## サンフランシスコのリバイバルは続く
REVIVAL AT SAN FRANCISCO CONTINUES

To Brother Brinkham and Readers of The Pentecostal Herald:
ブリンカム兄弟と『ペンテコスタル・ヘラルド』誌の読者のみなさんへ

San Francisco has had a really great spiritual awakening. People who have long resided here claim that the greatest revival that this city has ever known has visited it during the past two months. Sister Woodworth-Etter continued here about seven weeks in active campaign work. The last two weeks of this special effort the meetings were held in the National Theater, with a seating capacity of 1,500. On the last Sunday night it was estimated that about that number were present during the meeting. The power of God fell in a marked degree at nearly every meeting. The mark of a genuine revival is this, that after the evangelists are gone the revival continues. That feature is practically true in this case. Let the Coming King receive all the homage and the glory, for it belongeth unto Him. People in various parts, whom we never even met. were either healed or saved, or both, in this revival. The meetings go forward grandly. A splendid hall, seating about 750 people, is now being specially fitted up for the Pentecostal work in this city. It will be located at 1636 Ellis Street, near Filmore Street.
サンフランシスコは、実に大きな霊的覚醒を経験した。長くここに住んでいる人々は、この町がかつて知った最大のリバイバルが、この二か月のあいだに訪れたと言っている。ウッドワース・エター姉妹は、およそ七週間ここにとどまり、精力的に伝道の働きをした。この特別伝道の最後の二週間は、千五百人を収容するナショナル劇場で集会が開かれた。最後の日曜の夜には、ほぼその人数が集会に集まっていたと見積もられた。ほとんどすべての集会で、神の力が際立って下った。
真のリバイバルのしるしは、伝道者たちが去った後もリバイバルが続くことである。今回は、実際にそのとおりになっている。来たるべき王が、すべての敬意と栄光をお受けになるように。それは主のものだからである。私たちが会ったこともない各地の人々が、このリバイバルでいやされたり救われたり、あるいはその両方を受けたりした。集会は盛んに続いている。七百五十人ほどが座れるりっぱな会館が、今、この町のペンテコステの働きのために特別に整えられている。それはフィルモア通りの近く、エリス通り一六三六番地に置かれる。
// 英文の「met. were」は「met, were」、「Filmore」は「Fillmore」と読んだ。

It is strongly believed that could Sister Etter have continued for two months longer, the Lord would have shaken the whole city, and since she has gone we expect God to do it, anyway.
エター姉妹があと二か月続けることができたなら、主は町全体を揺り動かされただろうと、強く信じられている。姉妹は去ったが、それでも私たちは、神がそうしてくださることを期待している。

The manifestations of the Spirit were inspirational, and many times God came forth in solemn warning of the Master's imminent return, or in calling attention to sins, and weights in the lives of the people who should be stripped for the race.
御霊の現れは、人々を奮い立たせた。神はたびたびお現れになって、主の来臨が間近であることを厳粛に警告なさった。また、競走のために脱ぎ捨てるべき、人々の生活の中の罪と重荷に、目を向けさせられた。

Heaven's recording angel has the record preserved of the large number who were saved and baptized in the Holy Spirit with the sign following.
救われて、伴うしるしと共に聖霊のバプテスマを受けた大勢の人々の記録は、天の記録の御使が保管している。

Our hearts are filled with thanksgiving and gladness that after standing in our lot at this point for three and a half years God is so pouring out His Spirit.
私たちはここで三年半、自分の持ち場に立ってきた。その後に、神がこれほど御霊を注いでくださっているので、私たちの心は感謝と喜びに満ちている。

ROBERT J. CRAIG, Pastor,
（署名）牧師　ロバート・J・クレイグ

## 油を注がれたハンカチによっていやされる
HEALED THROUGH ANOINTED HANDKERCHIEF

During this meeting a handkerchief was sent to Canada (Ayton, Ont.), where a woman was almost dead with stomach trouble. Medicines and doctors could do nothing more for her, as she was rapidly failing in strength. Hearing of the work God was doing she sent for a handkerchief and had it anointed. When she applied it to her body she soon felt the healing virtue, and not long after came the report that she had the victory, was up and around and could eat again.
この集会のあいだに、一枚のハンカチがカナダ（オンタリオ州エイトン）に送られた。そこでは、ひとりの女性が胃の病気で死にかけていた。体力が急速に衰えていたので、薬も医者も、もう彼女のために何もできなかった。神がなさっている働きを聞いて、彼女はハンカチを送ってくれるよう頼み、それに油を注いでもらった。それを体に当てると、間もなくいやしの力を感じた。そしてほどなく、彼女が勝利を得て、起き上がって動き回り、また食べられるようになったという知らせが届いた。

## 御霊によって病の床から起こされる
'RAISED FROM SICKNESS BY THE SPIRIT

Another sister was sick in bed. While looking to God to strengthen her so that she could go to the meeting, the Spirit raised her up. She ran about nine blocks. On the way to the meeting she found a sick man on the street and brought him along. This man was about to go to a hospital for an operation. He also was deaf and had bad, running sores on his leg. God saved and healed him.

God snatched some individuals from the very strongholds of satan. A Miss P. Vogel, a leader of the I. W. W.'s, called by them the "Queen of the Unemployed," got saved and baptized. She has lectured to thousands, and speaks eight different languages. Has been in the Indian and Chinese revolutions. Her career has been wonderful in her short life. Many of the saints in the various missions in San Francisco have prayed for this woman. While she was honest in heart she could not get rid of her anarchistic spirit. In these meetings she saw the power of God demonstrated, and accompanied with signs, like she never saw before. The Spirit began to work on her and brought her under deep conviction. While this was going on the police began to connect her name with the present Chinese revolutionary spirits in the city.

The saints invited her out to the "Home of Peace" in Oakland to pray and tarry with her. She soon experienced salvation and then received the Holy Ghost, and began to speak in other languages. She has since interceded for hours in the Spirit for others. Reports come continually that she is out and out for God; that she longs to get out and preach that Jesus is the Living Christ.

One evening (the church being crowded) a boy about 12 years old was prayed for. He began to speak in other tongues and interpret. Suddenly a man burst forth from the gallery, saying: "God must be an awful wicked God for permitting such a spell to be put on a child." The man that made the disturbance was J. E. M., called the "Revolutionary Poet," of the labor movement of America. With him were a number of his friends. They actually thought a spell had been put over the child. Later some of them acknowledged that it was some other power. Prayer was offered for them. Some one got under deep conviction and made inquiries about salvation. He had such a restless spirit in him, that he could not bear to be where the saints prayed. He struggled and put off till the last day of the meeting, when Sister Etter and a few of the workers prayed for him. He at once testified that he felt much better. With his six-year-old girl he went into the tarrying room to seek God. As he came in, the power fell on his little girl and she came out speaking in other tongues. This broke his heart. Soon the power fell on him, and in less than an hour after he was prayed for, he lay back in the tarrying room, praising God in another language.

God is now using both him and the girl in the work.

The last day a flaming sword was seen hanging over the pulpit. The sword of the Spirit is still doing its work. Since leaving, reports come that people get saved, healed and baptized right along. As many as four receiving their baptism in one day. Let us all praise and magnify God for his wonderful works unto the children of men.

AUGUST FEICK,

Ayton, Ontario, Canada.
