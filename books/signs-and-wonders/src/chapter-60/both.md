# 第六十章　一九一六年三月、テキサス州ヒューストンでのしるしと不思議
CHAPTER LX
THE SIGNS AND WONDERS AT HOUSTON, TEXAS, MARCH, 1916.

We were very sorry to close the meeting at San Francisco, for the convincing and awakening power was spreading in all the churches over the city. Many said if the meetings could have continued a few weeks longer, they believed thousands would have been brought to Jesus. We felt that our work was not done in that city, nor in many places in the State, so we all felt that in the near future God would send us back.
サンフランシスコの集会を閉じるのは、とても残念だった。人を納得させ目覚めさせる力が、町じゅうのすべての教会に広がりつつあったからである。集会があと数週間続けられたなら、何千人もの人がイエスのもとに導かれただろうと信じている、と多くの人が言った。私たちは、あの町でも、州の多くの所でも、私たちの働きは終わっていないと感じていた。それで私たちはみな、近いうちに神が私たちをまたお遣わしになるだろうと感じた。

We started on the long journey over the plains and mountains, the Lord wonderfully strengthened us. When we arrived in Houston, Texas, we were met by Brother Morwood (the pastor), for the first time, who with a few saints had made a brave effort to prepare for the largest Pentecostal convention that had ever been held in the city and praise the Lord they were not disappointed; for when we closed he, with many others, told a large audience, that the meetings were successful in every way, much more than he expected: that miracles of healing and signs of the presence of God was far beyond anything he had ever seen.
私たちは平原と山々を越える長い旅に出た。主は驚くほど私たちを力づけてくださった。テキサス州ヒューストンに着くと、モーウッド兄弟（牧師）が私たちを迎えてくれた。会うのは初めてだった。彼はわずかな聖徒たちと共に、この町でかつて開かれた最大のペンテコステの大会に備えようと、勇敢に努力していた。主をほめたたえよ。彼らは失望しなかった。集会を閉じるとき、彼はほかの多くの人々と共に、大勢の会衆に向かってこう語ったのである。集会はあらゆる点で成功で、彼が期待していたよりもはるかに大きかった。いやしの奇跡と神のご臨在のしるしは、彼がこれまでに見たどんなものよりもはるかにまさっていた、と。

Brother Ritchey and son, from Zion City, Ill., were present to help in most of the meetings; also Brother Caruthers was a blessing to all and encouraged me. From the first service till the last, they brought their sick, lame, blind, deaf and dumb; some on beds; some on crutches; others carried in, but there was none too hard for the Lord. Every one that came in God's divine way was made to shout and praise the Lord for healing.
イリノイ州ザイオンシティーから来たリッチー兄弟とその息子が、ほとんどの集会に出て助けてくれた。またカラザーズ兄弟も、すべての人にとって祝福となり、私を励ましてくれた。最初の集いから最後まで、人々は病人、足の不自由な人、目の見えない人、耳が聞こえず口のきけない人を連れて来た。寝床のまま運ばれて来る人もいれば、松葉杖をついて来る人もいた。かつぎ込まれる人もいた。しかし、主にとって難しすぎる者はひとりもなかった。神の定められた方法で来た人はみな、いやされて叫び、主をほめたたえずにはいられなかった。

A man and wife came from South Dakota, two thousand or more miles. He was given up by all the best doctors; could hardly live coming on the train. They told me there is a man which was brought a long distance, who is nearly dead in the camp. I told them to bring him on the platform. He soon got saved, healed and filled with heavenly virtue, and resurrection life. He arose, walked and praised God. His wife also got saved and healed and baptized. They were Lutherans and had never seen any demonstrations of the Spirit. When his wife began to seek the baptism he told her if she would fall on the floor under that power, he would go home and leave her. While at the altar she was struck down. The sisters stood around her so that he could not see her. When they looked around they saw him lying in another part of the house, under the mighty power of the Lord, having visions. When both he and his wife arose from the Power they testified that God is surely visiting His people; that they got saved, healed and filled with the Holy Ghost. On the way home they testified at every opportunity what they saw and heard, and what God had done for them. When they got home they stirred up the whole neighborhood, so that a request came back for a meeting, saying we must have one, for we are hungry for the deep things of God.
ひとりの男とその妻が、二千マイル以上も離れたサウスダコタからやって来た。彼は最も優れた医者たちにみな見放されていた。列車で来るあいだも、生きているのがやっとだった。人々は私に、遠くから運ばれて来た男の人が会場で死にかけていると告げた。私は、彼を壇に連れて来るように言った。彼は間もなく救われ、いやされ、天の力と復活のいのちに満たされた。彼は起き上がり、歩いて、神をほめたたえた。妻も救われ、いやされ、バプテスマを受けた。
// 「in the camp」の camp は集会の場所を指すと見て「会場」と訳した。
ふたりはルター派の信者で、御霊の現れを見たことが一度もなかった。妻がバプテスマを求めはじめると、彼は妻に、もしあの力の下で床に倒れたりしたら、置いて家に帰ってしまうぞ、と言った。祭壇にいるあいだに、彼女は打ち倒された。姉妹たちは、彼に見えないよう彼女の周りに立った。ところが、ふと見回すと、彼が会場の別の所で主の大いなる力の下に倒れ、幻を見ていた。ふたりとも力の下から起き上がると、こう証しをした。神は確かにご自分の民を顧みてくださっている。自分たちは救われ、いやされ、聖霊に満たされた、と。
帰る道々、ふたりは機会あるごとに、見たこと聞いたこと、神が自分たちのためにしてくださったことを証しした。家に着くと、ふたりは近所じゅうを奮い立たせた。そのため、集会を開いてほしいという願いが送られて来た。ぜひ集会を開いてほしい、私たちは神の深いことに飢えているのです、というのである。

A railroad conductor and wife from Iowa, came to the meeting. He had been hurt in a wreck, so that he was crippled in the spine and other places. He had been laid off many months from his work. His wife was an invalid of long standing. When he saw the mighty works of God, he said that half had not been told. He knew there was a living merciful Christ; he wanted to get saved and healed, and was not disappointed. His spine was soon made straight and strong. His crooked, partially paralyzed limbs were made straight. He left his wife at the meeting as he went home. Soon one letter after another came back, telling how God had saved and healed him.
アイオワ州から、鉄道の車掌とその妻が集会に来た。彼は列車事故でけがをして、脊椎やほかの所が不自由になっていた。何か月も仕事を休まされていた。妻は長いあいだ病人だった。神の力あるみわざを見て、彼は、聞いていたことは半分にも満たなかったと言った。生きておられるあわれみ深いキリストがおられることを、彼は知った。彼は救われ、いやされたいと願い、その願いはかなえられた。彼の脊椎は間もなくまっすぐに、強くなった。曲がって半ばまひしていた手足もまっすぐになった。彼は妻を集会に残して家に帰った。間もなく次々と手紙が届き、神がどのように彼を救ってくださり、いやしてくださったかを伝えてきた。
// 「half had not been told」は列王上一〇・七の借用。

A woman was brought on a cot, who had been very low for many weeks, starving to death. Nothing would stay on her stomach. They carried her from a back room to the platform. We put her in the arms of Jesus, covered her with the Blood. In a few minutes she arose, walked and shouted, shaking hands and praising God. She stayed for the night meeting, and came to almost every meeting, helping others, singing in the Spirit, telling how God had healed her. As soon as she got healed, she said, "Wire to my children that I am healed and am hungry and can eat now."
簡易寝台に乗せられて運ばれて来た女性がいた。彼女は何週間もひどく弱っていて、飢え死にしかけていた。何も胃に収まらなかったのである。人々は彼女を奥の部屋から壇に運んだ。私たちは彼女をイエスの腕にゆだね、血でおおった。数分のうちに彼女は起き上がり、歩いて叫び、握手をして神をほめたたえた。彼女は夜の集会まで残った。そしてほとんどすべての集会に来て、ほかの人々を助け、御霊によって歌い、神がどのように自分をいやしてくださったかを語った。いやされるとすぐに、彼女は言った。「子供たちに電報を打ってください。私はいやされて、おなかがすいていて、もう食べられますって」

A woman was brought from her bed in her night gown, carried in like a helpless child, all bloated from head to foot with dropsy, she had been operated on several times; the doctors said she could not live to have another operation. She was healed in the presence of all, like they all are. She came day after day, sitting up and walking; the swelling going down and getting stronger. Cancers, tumors, Bright's disease, rupture and almost every disease, and plagues of the worst kind were healed. The blind, the deaf and dumb were made to see, hear and sing by the power of God. The heavenly choir, spiritual dancing, strong messages in other tongues, interpretations and prophecies were given accompanied with the presence of God; this made the sinners and saints tremble.
寝間着のまま寝床から連れて来られた女性もいた。彼女は体の自由のきかない子供のように運び込まれた。水腫のために、頭から足まですっかりむくんでいた。何度も手術を受けており、医者たちは、もう一度手術を受けたら助からないだろうと言っていた。ほかのすべての人と同じように、彼女もみなの前でいやされた。彼女は毎日やって来て、座ったり歩いたりした。むくみは引いていき、彼女は強くなっていった。
がん、腫瘍、ブライト病、脱腸、そしてほとんどあらゆる病と、最も悪い種類の疫病がいやされた。目の見えない人、耳が聞こえず口のきけない人が、神の力によって、見え、聞こえ、歌えるようになった。天の合唱、霊的な踊り、異言による力強い言葉、解き明かし、預言が、神のご臨在を伴って与えられた。それは罪人も聖徒も震えさせた。

We always have the best of order. People are afraid to laugh and mock in the presence of God. We never allow fanaticism, or other spirits, contentions or doctrines to be aired in the meetings. Everything that God does through the Holy Ghost is edifying, and the best of order is always maintained. So every honest person will have to admit that it is God working through His people.
私たちの集会は、いつもきわめて秩序正しい。人々は神のご臨在の前で、笑ったりあざけったりするのを恐れる。私たちは、狂信や、ほかの霊、争い、教理が集会で持ち出されるのを決して許さない。神が聖霊を通してなさることはすべて徳を高めるものであり、いつも最善の秩序が保たれる。だから、正直な人はみな、それは神がご自分の民を通して働いておられるのだと認めないわけにはいかないだろう。

The Spirit of love is felt and seen in all and through all. So that they will say, "Of a truth God is here." Brother Morwood is a lovely Christian man. He stood like a brave soldier and was very kind to me. His wife and all the band did all they could to help me and make me comfortable.
愛の御霊が、すべての人のうちに、すべての人を通して感じられ、見られる。それで人々は、「まことに、神がここにおられる」と言うのである。モーウッド兄弟は、すばらしいクリスチャンの男性である。彼は勇敢な兵士のように立ち、私にとても親切にしてくれた。彼の妻も、団の人々もみな、私を助け、快く過ごせるよう、できる限りのことをしてくれた。
// 「Of a truth God is here」は第一コリント一四・二五の借用。

Now, after some months, we just received a letter from him telling how the interest was deepening. They have had a meeting every night since we left. Many were saved and baptized, and some great healings were wrought in the Name of the Lord.
数か月たった今、彼から手紙を受け取ったばかりである。関心がますます深まっていることが書かれていた。私たちが去ってから、毎晩集会が開かれているという。多くの人が救われ、バプテスマを受けた。主の御名によって、いくつかの大きないやしも行われた。

May the good work ever go on.
この良い働きが、いつまでも続くように。

Sister Etter is, without doubt, one of the greatest evangelists of today. Houston, Tex., March 18, 1916.

I am glad to report great success in Houston. The papers have taken notice and have written it up. The people cannot understand what it is; they have left God out so long that they don't recognize Him when He makes His appearance. The conditions in Houston were favorable for a revival. There were no serious divisions and the saints were working in harmony, so the Lord could manifest Himself from the very start. Outsiders are becoming interested and many who have never heard of Pentecost before are becoming hungry for the experience. About the first thing that took place was a holiness preacher got the baptism and spoke in tongues as the Spirit gave utterance. He says he used to preach himself under conviction when he preached on the baptism of the Holy Spirit, but now that he has the genuine experience he can preach it with greater power.

The writer has known Sister Etter for many years and can say that her faith is as strong today as it was when she was a young woman, and that her power of physical endurance is even greater than in former years. Bro. E. N. Ritchie, pastor of the Bruner tabernacle, this city, testified in this meeting to being healed of leakage of the heart in Sister Etter's meeting at Decatur, Ill., thirty years ago.

Jesus is coming soon and is bringing His reward with Him. Therefore, we should press the battle to the gates, trusting Him to be with us; and with many other words encouraging His people to work for the salvation of those who are lost.

We are greatly encouraged, for we know that God is with us. Praise the Lord.

Your brother in Christ,

F. A. Hate.

## 少女の夢
A LITTLE GIRL'S DREAM

Dear Sister Etter: I dreamed I saw the prettiest angels up in the sky. They looked so pretty in their white robes. It looked like as if each one of them had a long trumpet in their mouth. Then I saw Christ up in the clouds in my dream. Some people seemed to be afraid and wanted to run from Him, but I said, "Stand still, He will do you no harm." I think this is a warning to the people to get right with God.
愛するエター姉妹へ　わたしは、空の上にとてもきれいな御使たちがいる夢を見ました。白い衣を着ていて、とてもきれいでした。御使たちはひとりひとり、長いラッパを口に当てているように見えました。それから夢の中で、雲の上にキリストが見えました。キリストを恐れて、逃げ出そうとしている人たちもいるようでした。でもわたしは、「動かないで。あの方は何も悪いことはなさらないわ」と言いました。これは、人々が神との関係を正しくするようにという警告だと思います。

I want to thank God for what He has done for me during this meeting. I have had sciatica for thirty-five years, and for the last fifteen years have been confined to the house, and a good deal of the time to bed; but I can now truthfully say that I am healed. When Sister Etter laid hands on me and prayed the Lord took all the pains away. My age is 76. Glory to God!

## 足の不自由な人が歩けるようになる
LAME MADE TO WALK

（ここには、L・C・ネルソン夫人の証しが載っているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）
// ここから住所の行「1218 Washington St.」までは、Mrs. L. C. Nelson の証し（日付の行を含む）。WebSearch（standard 1〜2 回）で没年が見つからなかったので訳さない。
Houston, Tex., March 23, 1916.

I am so glad I can join my testimony in with others to praise God for His mighty healing power.

I was about two years old when I had the slow fever and it settled in my left limb and made it shorter than the other, and after I got up and could walk around I cut my foot very badly on a piece of glass, which severed the leaders and caused my foot to grow crooked. And I have found it very hard to walk around and to do my work.

I have been saved and baptized in the Holy Spirit almost six years; and have trusted God that some day I would get healed. But I did not get a real touch for healing until I went to the meeting that Sister Etter was holding in the city. And on the 19th day of March God wonderfully touched me. Praise His dear name! I was standing on the platform singing when I felt the Spirit of God moving upon me. Sister Etter was sitting in a chair just a little way from me. There was no healing service at the time, but the Spirit said to me, "Step over to her," and I obeyed. She said God showed her He wanted to pour out His Spirit on me, and she put one hand on me and told me to look up; and as I did so I felt the power of God go through my limb from my hip to my toes. Not until then did Sister Etter notice that I was crippled. Then laying both hands on my shoulders she said, "Jesus heals you now." And then the power went through my limbs like an electric shock and this set me to leaping and praising God.

From that time until this my limb has begun to grow longer and stronger, and my foot to straighten out. Bless His name forever.

MRS. L. C. NELSON,

1218 Washington St.

## 嗅覚が戻った驚くべき例
MARVELOUS CASE OF SENSE OF SMELL RESTORED

（ここには、A・J・ファウス夫人の証しが載っているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）
// ここから住所の行「1218 Washington Ave.」までは、Mrs. A. J. Fauss の証し（日付の行を含む）。WebSearch（standard 1〜2 回）で没年が見つからなかったので訳さない。
Houston, Tex., March 19, 1916.

On the 14th of March I was healed of an offensive sore in my nose. It would get worse in the winter time, and I never had any sense of smell since I can remember. I could not even smell the worst odors nor the fragrance of the beautiful roses. I would see people smelling flowers and talking about them, and I would wish I could smell them too. My parents thought I had catarrh and spent a great deal of money on patent medicines trying to get a cure for me, but nothing did me any good.

When I was three years old I had the measles, and when I got almost well I took cold and had a backset and my parents thought the trouble started from that time. As I grew up they found out that I could not smell at all; and it would be so embarrassing at times, when some one would put something under my nose to smell and I would have to tell them I could not smell. To prove to one and all that I could not smell, I will tell you what happened when I was a little girl. I went out in the fields one day and ran across a beautiful little white striped animal. I killed it and carried it home to give to my mother. When my mother smelled the odor and saw the pole-cat in my hands, she made me drop it pretty quick and took me out in the barnyard and gave me a bath and change of dress. I had a big nose but no sense of smell. Some would laugh at me, and many times I would make out like I could smell, to keep people from laughing at me. But when I got saved from sin on April 7, 1905, I could not deceive people that way any more.

So on last Tuesday night I was healed. Sister Etter laid her hands on my nose, and when she prayed for me I instantly felt the healing stream of Jesus go through my nose. I shouted the victory. Then the devil said to me, "How do you know you can smell?" I opened my eyes and right in front of me was a woman with a bouquet of roses pinned on her dress. You know God provided the sacrifice for Abraham just at the time he needed it; and so He provided the roses for me just when I needed them. I asked the sister for the roses and she handed them to me, and for the first time in my life I smelled the sweet fragrance of God's beautiful rose. Oh, such a wave of glory went through and through my very being. Words can not express the joy that I felt. This is not the first time I have been healed by God's mighty power. Husband and I and five children trust God for both soul and body, for which I praise Him and give Him all the glory.

MRS. A. J. FAUSS,

1218 Washington Ave.

## 神経衰弱がいやされる
HEALED OF NERVOUS PROSTRATION

（ここには、イーノック・ゴードン夫人の証しが載っているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）
// ここから署名「MRS. ENOCH GORDON.」までは、Mrs. Enoch Gordon の証し（日付の行と小見出し「Doctor's Advice」を含む）。WebSearch（standard 1〜2 回）で没年が見つからなかったので訳さない。
Kingsville, Tex., March, 1916.

Will send in my testimony that the Lord may be glorified, and in hopes that some poor afflicted one may seek Jesus as their healer. For three years had been a complete invalid. Greater part of the time being confined to the bed. "Neurasthenia," or nervous prostration, affecting the motor nerves principally at intervals of three, four and seven months, being bedfast, unable to walk at all. After trying masseurs, osteopaths, electric massages and for three months the live nerve cells of animals injected in the hip. Still I could not walk.

Doctor's Advice

My doctor at this time, being a devout man, and fully persuaded that I'd never walk, unless God undertook for me, advised me to obey James 5:14-15, which I did; being enabled to walk to and from a table three or four times a day; a distance of about one dozen steps, and to drive, several times a week for past nine months, but no more was possible. But I knew the Lord had undertaken for me and was dealing with me. Hearing of the Etter meeting going to be in Houston, I undertook the trip with great difficulty. I knew the Lord was going to bless me. I prayed earnestly that there would be some physical demonstration in my body when the disease was rebuked, but there was no such sign given me. I had to take God's words on simple faith and obey.

After the disease was rebuked was able to walk three and four blocks every day, while in Houston, attending both afternoon and night services for a week.

While I'm not in a normal condition as yet, I know that the death stroke has been applied to the disease, and the effects will have to leave sooner or later, and am confident the "Lord will give me to possess every bit of ground I set my foot on."

Money could not replace the joyful experiences of my Houston trip. I also received a glorious anointing. Please pray for me that I may see the Bible evidence of the baptism "speaking in tongues" soon.

God be with you, bless and continue to confirm the word as you preach.

MRS. ENOCH GORDON.

## 一八八八年に異言を語る
SPOKE IN TONGUES, 1888

（ここには、A・P・デニスの手紙が載っているが、書き手の没年が確かめられないため訳していない）

A. P. Dennis, of Scottsburg, Ind., writes as follows:

"Attending a meeting conducted by the evangelist, M. B. Woodworth (now Woodworth-Etter) in the fall of 1886, I received a wonderful anointing and enduement of power. I had been saved nine months previous to this experience. From this time on, I labored for Jesus, and He honored us with many souls. Sometimes I have seen a roomful slain like men in battle under the mighty power of God.

"One night in October, 1888, while many were slain, I became as a drunken man. I began to stutter, or stammer, and then suddenly began speaking in other tongues. Always after this there seemed to be a greater anointing upon me, but I concluded that it was only a strange operation of the Spirit. In 1912 I attended a camp-meeting conducted by Brother Haywood in Indianapolis, Ind., on purpose to receive the baptism in the Holy Spirit. I received Him in great glory and spake in tongues. Then I knew that it was the same that I had received in 1888.

"Since then my wife has gone home; an angel, or white form standing at the foot of the bed, saying, 'I am sent for thy wife.' Before leaving, she asked many questions about the 'bright ones' about her.

"T now see many lonely days, but Jesus seems more precious than ever before. I had thought she would remain with me till Jesus came, 'but the dead in Christ shall rise first, then we who are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air; and so shall we ever be with the Lord."
